Tofu Scramble
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Crumbled firm tofu cooked in a skillet with onion, spices, and sometimes turmeric for a breakfast that stands in for scrambled eggs. It is a savory vegan option.

SERVINGS
2 to 3

TOTAL TIME
About 20 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A large skillet or saute pan
- A heat-safe spatula or wooden spoon
- A splatter screen

INGREDIENTS
- 14 ounces firm tofu, drained
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1/4 cup (a quarter cup) diced onion
- 1/4 teaspoon (a quarter teaspoon) salt, plus more to taste
- 1/4 teaspoon turmeric, optional
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder or black pepper
- 1 cup spinach or leftover vegetables, optional

TACTILE TIPS
Firm tofu feels like a moist, springy block. After you crumble it, it should feel like large curds, not a paste.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Drain the tofu and pat it dry. Drain means pour off the liquid.

2. Crumble it into a bowl with your fingers.

3. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat.

4. Add the onion and a pinch of salt. A pinch is a small amount you can hold between two fingers, about 1/8 teaspoon (an eighth of a teaspoon).

5. Cook 3 to 4 minutes, until it feels soft.

6. Add the tofu, turmeric, garlic powder or pepper, and the remaining salt.

7. Cook, stirring, 6 to 8 minutes, until the tofu feels hot and slightly drier at the edges.

8. Add spinach or vegetables if you like, and cook until they are hot and any spinach is wilted.

9. Taste a cooled bite and add salt if it needs it.

10. Serve hot.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The tofu should feel hot and slightly firm at the edges, not cold and watery. You should smell onion and toasting soy, not burning.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The tofu should look a little golden in spots. If you used turmeric, it will look yellow, like scrambled eggs. It should not look wet and pale.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The tofu should look a little golden in spots. If you used turmeric, it will look yellow, like scrambled eggs. It should not look wet and pale.

SAFETY
Use a clean towel to press tofu. The skillet is hot. Leftovers should be refrigerated and reheated to 165 degrees F. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
A splash of soy sauce or a pinch of kala namak (sulfur salt) can add a more egg-like smell if you have it. Do not over-mash the tofu.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
