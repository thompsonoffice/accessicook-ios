ACCESSICOOK
Cream of Wheat
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A smooth hot cereal made from wheat farina. It cooks quickly and can stick if you walk away. Stir as it thickens.

SERVINGS
2

TOTAL TIME
About 10 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A large pot with a lid
- A wooden spoon or ladle

INGREDIENTS
- 2 1/2 cups (two and a half cups) water or milk, or a mix
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) Cream of Wheat or plain farina
- A pinch of salt
- 1 tablespoon butter or brown sugar, optional

TACTILE TIPS
Dry farina feels finer than grits, like very fine sand. The finished cereal should feel silky, not lumpy and not pasty.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Bring the liquid and salt to a boil.

2. Slowly whisk in the farina. Whisk means beat with a fork or a whisk until the mixture looks even.

3. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring, 2 to 3 minutes, until it is thick and smooth.

4. Stir in butter or sugar if you like.

5. Serve at once.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: It should feel silky and thick enough to hold a trail from a spoon. You should smell cooked wheat, not a scorched bottom.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: It should look smooth and pale, with no dry lumps.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. It should look smooth and pale, with no dry lumps.

SAFETY
It thickens fast and can spatter. Stay with the pot. Reheat leftovers with extra liquid until steaming. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
If lumps form, whisk hard or pass the mix through a strainer. A pinch of cinnamon is a good topping. A pinch is a small amount you can hold between two fingers, about 1/8 teaspoon (an eighth of a teaspoon).

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
