Cottage Cheese Breakfast Bowl
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A no-cook bowl of cottage cheese with fruit, nuts, or a drizzle of honey. It is a fast, high-protein breakfast.

SERVINGS
1

TOTAL TIME
About 5 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 1 cup cottage cheese
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) fruit, such as pineapple, berries, or sliced peach
- 2 tablespoons nuts or granola, optional
- 1 teaspoon honey, optional

TACTILE TIPS
Cottage cheese feels curdy and moist. If it is very watery, drain a little off. Drain means pour off the liquid. Fruit should feel ripe and smell fresh.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Spoon the cottage cheese into a bowl.

2. Add the fruit, nuts or granola, and honey if you like.

3. Serve at once, while the crunchy toppings are still crisp.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The cottage cheese should smell fresh and clean, not sour in a sharp way. You should feel cool curds and soft fruit.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The fruit should look fresh, not brown and dull. The cottage cheese should look moist, not dried out or pink or moldy. If it looks or smells off, throw it out.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The fruit should look fresh, not brown and dull. The cottage cheese should look moist, not dried out or pink or moldy. If it looks or smells off, throw it out.

SAFETY
Keep cottage cheese refrigerated and check the date. Do not leave the bowl out more than 2 hours. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Savory version: skip honey and fruit, and add salt, pepper, cucumber, and tomatoes instead.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
