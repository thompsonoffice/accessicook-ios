Yogurt Parfait
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A simple layered bowl of yogurt, fruit, and granola or cereal. There is no cooking. It is a good first recipe when you want something cold and fast.

SERVINGS
1

TOTAL TIME
About 5 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 1 cup yogurt
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) fruit, such as berries or sliced banana
- 1/3 cup (a third of a cup) granola or other cereal
- 1 teaspoon honey, optional

TACTILE TIPS
Layer so you get a little of each thing in a spoonful. Granola feels crunchy. If it sits in yogurt too long it softens.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Spoon half the yogurt into a glass or bowl.

2. Add half the fruit and half the granola.

3. Repeat the layers.

4. Drizzle honey on top if you like.

5. Serve at once.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: You should feel cool yogurt, soft fruit, and crunchy granola. The yogurt should smell fresh, not sour in a sharp way. If the yogurt smells off, throw it out.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: You should see distinct layers, or a simple mix if you prefer it stirred. The fruit should look fresh, not slimy.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. You should see distinct layers, or a simple mix if you prefer it stirred. The fruit should look fresh, not slimy.

SAFETY
Keep yogurt refrigerated until you build the parfait. Wash the fruit. Use the granola within a reasonable time after opening, and do not use it if it smells rancid. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Build it just before you eat so the granola stays crisp. See Granola in this chapter if you want to make your own.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
