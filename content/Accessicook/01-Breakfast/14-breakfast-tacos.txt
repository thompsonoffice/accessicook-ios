ACCESSICOOK
Breakfast Tacos
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Soft tortillas filled with set scrambled eggs, cheese, and simple toppings. They are messier than a burrito and easier to fill in small amounts.

SERVINGS
2 to 3, about 6 tacos

TOTAL TIME
About 25 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A large skillet or saute pan
- A heat-safe spatula or wooden spoon
- A splatter screen
- A talking or instant-read food thermometer

INGREDIENTS
- 6 small corn or flour tortillas
- 5 large eggs
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) salt
- 3/4 cup (three-quarters of a cup) shredded cheese
- Optional toppings: salsa, leftover potatoes, or chopped cilantro

TACTILE TIPS
Warm tortillas are flexible. Cold corn tortillas crack. Keep toppings in small bowls so you can build each taco by feel.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Scramble the eggs with the salt in butter until they are set, 160 degrees F.

2. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet until they feel flexible.

3. Spoon eggs down the center of each tortilla.

4. Add cheese and any toppings.

5. Serve at once, while the tortillas are warm.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The eggs should feel set and the tortillas should feel warm and bendy. The filling should be hot.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The eggs should look set. The tortillas should look soft, with no hard cracks.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The eggs should look set. The tortillas should look soft, with no hard cracks.

SAFETY
Cook eggs until firm. Reheat leftover potatoes or meat to 165 degrees F before you add them. Cook egg dishes to 160 degrees F. There should be no liquid raw egg left.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Set out a plate under the taco as you eat, because fillings spill. A little cheese under the eggs can help the taco hold together.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
