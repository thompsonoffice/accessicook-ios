French Toast Casserole
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Cubed bread baked in an egg and milk mix, like French toast in a pan. You can assemble it the night before. Bake until the center reaches 160 degrees F.

SERVINGS
6 to 8

TOTAL TIME
About 55 minutes, or overnight plus baking time

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans
- A talking or instant-read food thermometer

INGREDIENTS
- 1 tablespoon butter, for the pan
- 8 cups cubed bread
- 8 large eggs
- 2 1/2 cups (two and a half cups) milk
- 1/3 cup (a third of a cup) sugar
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) salt

TACTILE TIPS
The bread should feel evenly moist before baking, with no dry cubes sitting on top.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oven to 350 degrees F, unless you are chilling the dish overnight.

2. Butter a 9 by 13 inch pan.

3. Spread the bread in the pan.

4. Beat the eggs, milk, sugar, vanilla, cinnamon, and salt.

5. Pour the mix over the bread.

6. Press the cubes down gently.

7. If you are baking later, cover and refrigerate overnight, then heat the oven to 350 degrees F.

8. Bake 35 to 45 minutes, until the center is set and a thermometer reads 160 degrees F, or 165 degrees F if the dish was refrigerated overnight.

9. Rest 10 minutes. Rest means let cooked meat sit so the juices settle back into the meat.

10. Serve warm.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The center should feel set, not liquid. A thermometer should read at least 160 degrees F. You should smell toasted bread and custard.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The top should be golden and puffed. The center should not look wet.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The top should be golden and puffed. The center should not look wet.

SAFETY
Raw egg is in the soak. Bake until the center is set. A chilled overnight casserole should reach 165 degrees F. Cook casseroles to 165 degrees F in the center.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Use slightly stale bread. Add raisins or berries on top before baking if you like.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
