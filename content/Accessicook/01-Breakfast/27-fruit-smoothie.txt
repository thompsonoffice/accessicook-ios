Fruit Smoothie
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A blended drink of fruit, yogurt or milk, and optional ice. It is a fast cold breakfast. A blender is sharp and should be used with the lid on.

SERVINGS
1 to 2

TOTAL TIME
About 5 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork
- A blender, with the lid

INGREDIENTS
- 1 cup frozen or fresh fruit, such as berries or banana
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) yogurt or 1/2 cup milk
- 1/2 cup juice or more milk, as needed to blend
- 1 teaspoon honey, optional

TACTILE TIPS
Frozen fruit makes a thicker smoothie. The finished drink should feel cold and sip-able, not so thick that the blender struggles, and not as thin as juice.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Put the liquid in the blender first, then the yogurt, then the fruit.

2. Put the lid on firmly.

3. Blend until the mix feels even and you no longer hear large chunks knocking, 20 to 40 seconds.

4. Stop the blender.

5. Taste a small sip.

6. Add honey or more liquid if you want it sweeter or thinner.

7. Pour and serve at once.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The sound will change from knocking chunks to a smooth whirl. The drink should feel even on the tongue, with no hard ice chunks unless you like them.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The smoothie should look even in color, with no large fruit pieces left, unless you prefer it chunky.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The smoothie should look even in color, with no large fruit pieces left, unless you prefer it chunky.

SAFETY
Always use the lid. Do not put your hand in the blender jar. Unplug the blender before you reach a spatula near the blade. Wash the blade carefully. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
A frozen banana makes the mix creamier. If the blender stalls, add liquid and start again on low.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
