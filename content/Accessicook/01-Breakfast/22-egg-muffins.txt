Egg Muffins
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Eggs baked in a muffin pan with cheese and cooked vegetables or meat. They are handy to reheat on a busy morning. Bake them to 160 degrees F.

SERVINGS
6 muffins, 3 to 6 servings

TOTAL TIME
About 30 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans
- A talking or instant-read food thermometer

INGREDIENTS
- 6 large eggs
- 1/4 cup (a quarter cup) milk
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) salt
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) shredded cheese
- 1/2 cup chopped cooked vegetables or cooked meat
- Oil spray or butter, for the pan

TACTILE TIPS
The cups should be well greased or the muffins will stick. Filling each cup about three-quarters full keeps them from overflowing.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oven to 350 degrees F.

2. Grease 6 muffin cups well.

3. Beat the eggs, milk, and salt.

4. Stir in the cheese and the cooked vegetables or meat.

5. Pour the mix into the cups, filling each about three-quarters full.

6. Bake 16 to 20 minutes, until the centers are set and a thermometer reads 160 degrees F.

7. Cool 5 minutes, then loosen the edges with a knife and lift them out.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The centers should feel springy, not liquid. A thermometer should read 160 degrees F. You should smell cooked egg, not burning cheese.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The muffins should look puffed and set, not wet in the center. They may settle as they cool.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The muffins should look puffed and set, not wet in the center. They may settle as they cool.

SAFETY
Use already-cooked meat, not raw sausage, in the mix. Grease the pan well. Use mitts. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Cook egg dishes to 160 degrees F. There should be no liquid raw egg left.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
These freeze well. Reheat from the fridge in a microwave until the center is 165 degrees F, or in a 325 degree F oven.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
