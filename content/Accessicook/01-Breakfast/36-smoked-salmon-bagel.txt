Smoked Salmon Bagel
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A bagel with cream cheese, smoked salmon, and optional onion or capers. The salmon is already cured and ready to eat from the package, as long as it has been kept cold.

SERVINGS
1

TOTAL TIME
About 10 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 1 bagel, split
- 2 tablespoons cream cheese
- 2 to 3 ounces sliced smoked salmon
- Optional: a few thin slices of red onion, capers, or a squeeze of lemon

TACTILE TIPS
Smoked salmon feels slippery, thin, and slightly oily. It is already sliced in most packs. Cream cheese should be soft enough to spread.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Split and toast the bagel if you like, as in Bagels with Cream Cheese.

2. Spread the cream cheese on both halves.

3. Lay the smoked salmon on top.

4. Add onion, capers, or lemon if you like.

5. Serve at once.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The salmon should feel cool and smell clean and smoky, not sour, fishy in a sharp way, or ammonia-like. If it smells off, throw it out.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The salmon should look moist and translucent pink, not dry, brown, or slimy. The bagel may be lightly golden if toasted.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The salmon should look moist and translucent pink, not dry, brown, or slimy. The bagel may be lightly golden if toasted.

SAFETY
Keep smoked salmon refrigerated until you use it. Wash your hands after handling it. Pregnant people and anyone with a weaker immune system should ask a doctor before eating cold-smoked fish. Cook fish to 145 degrees F, or until the flesh is opaque and flakes, meaning it comes apart easily with a fork.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Lox and nova-style salmon are used the same way. A squeeze of lemon brightens the saltiness.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
