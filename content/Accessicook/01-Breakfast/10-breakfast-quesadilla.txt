ACCESSICOOK
Breakfast Quesadilla
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A tortilla folded over cheese and set scrambled eggs, cooked in a skillet until the outside is crisp and the cheese is melted.

SERVINGS
2

TOTAL TIME
About 20 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A large skillet or saute pan
- A heat-safe spatula or wooden spoon
- A splatter screen
- A talking or instant-read food thermometer

INGREDIENTS
- 2 large flour tortillas
- 3 large eggs
- 1 tablespoon butter or oil, plus more for the pan
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- Salt
- Salsa, optional

TACTILE TIPS
The filling should sit on one half of the tortilla so you can fold it. Fold means gently turn the mixture over itself so you do not knock out the air. Do not overfill or the cheese will spill.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Scramble the eggs with a pinch of salt until they are set, 160 degrees F. A pinch is a small amount you can hold between two fingers, about 1/8 teaspoon (an eighth of a teaspoon).

2. Heat a skillet over medium heat and add a little butter or oil.

3. Lay a tortilla in the pan.

4. Sprinkle cheese on one half.

5. Add eggs.

6. Add a little more cheese.

7. Fold the empty half over the filling.

8. Cook 2 to 3 minutes per side, until the tortilla feels crisp and the cheese feels melted.

9. Move to a plate, cut into wedges, and serve with salsa if you like.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The tortilla should feel crisp. The center should feel hot and the cheese should feel soft and melted. You should smell toasting tortilla.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The outside should be golden with toasted spots. Cheese may peek out and look melted, not pale and solid.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The outside should be golden with toasted spots. Cheese may peek out and look melted, not pale and solid.

SAFETY
Cook eggs until set. The pan is hot. Use a spatula to turn the quesadilla so it does not unfold. Cook egg dishes to 160 degrees F. There should be no liquid raw egg left.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Leftover cooked vegetables can go in with the eggs. Keep the filling dry or the tortilla will steam instead of crisp.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
