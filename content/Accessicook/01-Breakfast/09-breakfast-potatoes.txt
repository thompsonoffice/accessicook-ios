ACCESSICOOK
Breakfast Potatoes
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Cubed potatoes cooked in a skillet until the outsides are crisp and the insides are tender. This is a simple side for eggs or a breakfast skillet.

SERVINGS
4

TOTAL TIME
About 35 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A large skillet or saute pan
- A heat-safe spatula or wooden spoon
- A splatter screen

INGREDIENTS
- 1 1/2 pounds (one and a half pounds) potatoes, cut into 1/2 inch (half inch) cubes
- 2 tablespoons oil
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) salt
- 1/4 teaspoon (a quarter teaspoon) black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) paprika or garlic powder, optional

TACTILE TIPS
Cut the cubes as even as you can so they finish together. A 1/2 inch cube is about the size of a dice. Dice means cut into small, even cubes.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Cut the potatoes into 1/2 inch cubes.

2. You can peel them or leave the skins on after a good wash.

3. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat.

4. Add the potatoes.

5. Sprinkle with salt, pepper, and paprika or garlic powder, if using.

6. Cook, stirring now and then, 15 to 20 minutes.

7. They are done when a cube mashes easily and the outsides feel crisp in spots.

8. Serve hot.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: A cube should mash easily and feel tender in the center. The outsides should feel crisp in spots. You should smell toasted potato, not burning or raw earthiness.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The potatoes should have golden-brown spots and look dry and crisp at the edges, not gray and steamed.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The potatoes should have golden-brown spots and look dry and crisp at the edges, not gray and steamed.

SAFETY
Use the claw grip and a cut-resistant glove. The oil can spatter. Use a splatter screen. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Cover the pan for the first 8 minutes if you want a softer start, then uncover to crisp. Leftover baked potatoes can be cubed and crisped in less time.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
