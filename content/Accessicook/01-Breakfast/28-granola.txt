ACCESSICOOK
Granola
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Oats baked with oil, sweetener, and nuts until crisp, then cooled and stored for yogurt or snacks. Stir during baking so the edges do not burn.

SERVINGS
About 8 cups, many servings

TOTAL TIME
About 45 minutes, plus cooling

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans

INGREDIENTS
- 4 cups rolled oats
- 1 1/2 cups (one and a half cups) chopped nuts or seeds
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) salt
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) oil, or melted butter
- 1/2 cup maple syrup or honey
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 cup dried fruit, added after baking

TACTILE TIPS
The mix should feel evenly damp with oil and syrup before it bakes. Finished granola should feel dry and crisp after it cools.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oven to 300 degrees F.

2. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment.

3. Mix the oats, nuts, cinnamon, and salt.

4. Stir in the oil, maple syrup or honey, and vanilla until everything feels lightly coated.

5. Spread the mix in an even layer.

6. Bake 30 to 40 minutes, stirring every 15 minutes, until the oats smell toasted and feel dry.

7. Cool completely on the pan. The granola crisps as it cools.

8. Stir in the dried fruit.

9. Store in a jar or a sealed bag.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: You should smell toasted oats and nuts, not burning. A cooled cluster should feel crisp, not chewy and wet.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The oats and nuts should look golden, not pale and raw, and not dark brown.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The oats and nuts should look golden, not pale and raw, and not dark brown.

SAFETY
Use mitts. Sugar in the mix can burn at the edges. Stir as directed. Let it cool before you bag it, or steam will soften it. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Add chocolate chips only after the granola is fully cool. If you like clusters, press the mix down before baking and stir less often.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
