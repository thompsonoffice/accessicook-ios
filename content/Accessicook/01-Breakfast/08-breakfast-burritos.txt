ACCESSICOOK
Breakfast Burritos
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Warm tortillas filled with scrambled eggs, cheese, and optional cooked sausage or beans. Cook the eggs until they are set. Warm any meat fillings until they are hot.

SERVINGS
4

TOTAL TIME
About 30 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A large skillet or saute pan
- A heat-safe spatula or wooden spoon
- A splatter screen
- A talking or instant-read food thermometer

INGREDIENTS
- 6 large eggs
- 1 tablespoon butter or oil
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) salt
- 4 large flour tortillas
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 1 cup cooked breakfast sausage or drained black beans, optional
- Salsa, optional

TACTILE TIPS
A flour tortilla should feel soft and flexible. If it cracks, warm it first. The filling should not be so wet that it soaks the wrap.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Scramble the eggs with the salt in butter until they are set and no longer liquid, as in the Scrambled Eggs recipe. They should reach 160 degrees F.

2. Warm the sausage or beans until they are steaming hot, 165 degrees F if they were leftover meat.

3. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet 20 seconds per side, or wrap them in a damp towel and microwave 20 to 30 seconds, until they feel flexible.

4. Lay a tortilla on a plate.

5. Add eggs, cheese, and any meat or beans down the center.

6. Fold in the sides, then roll from the bottom into a tight log. Fold means gently turn the mixture over itself so you do not knock out the air.

7. Serve with salsa if you like.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The eggs should feel set. The tortilla should feel warm and flexible. The filling should be hot.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The tortilla should look soft, with no dry cracks. The eggs should look set, not wet and raw.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The tortilla should look soft, with no dry cracks. The eggs should look set, not wet and raw.

SAFETY
Cook eggs to 160 degrees F. Reheat leftover meat to 165 degrees F. Do not use raw sausage as a filling. Cook egg dishes to 160 degrees F. There should be no liquid raw egg left.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
You can wrap burritos in foil and keep them in a 200 degree F oven for 15 minutes. For a crisp outside, brown the rolled burrito in a dry skillet 1 to 2 minutes per side.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
