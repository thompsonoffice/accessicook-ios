ACCESSICOOK
Baked Mac and Cheese
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Macaroni in a cheese sauce, baked until the top is browned and the center is hot. This is the oven version of the Stovetop Mac and Cheese in the Lunch chapter. A crumb topping is optional.

SERVINGS
6

TOTAL TIME
About 45 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans
- A talking or instant-read food thermometer

INGREDIENTS
- 1 pound elbow macaroni, cooked until slightly firmer than you want the finished dish, then drained
- 4 tablespoons butter
- 1/4 cup (a quarter cup) flour
- 3 cups milk
- 4 cups shredded cheddar cheese, divided
- 1 teaspoon salt, plus more to taste
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) paprika or black pepper
- Optional topping: 1/2 cup (half a cup) breadcrumbs tossed with 1 tablespoon melted butter

TACTILE TIPS
The sauce should feel smooth before it goes in the dish. The pasta should be slightly firm, because it will keep cooking in the oven. The baked center should feel hot and creamy, not dry and not soupy.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oven to 350 degrees F.

2. Grease the baking dish if the ingredients include butter or oil for the dish.

3. Make a sauce by cooking the butter and flour 1 minute, then whisking in the milk and cooking until slightly thick.

4. Take it off the heat and stir in most of the cheese, salt, and paprika or pepper.

5. Mix with the pasta and spread in a greased baking dish.

6. Add the remaining cheese and optional crumbs.

7. Bake 20 to 25 minutes.

8. The top should be bubbling and lightly browned, and the center should be steaming hot.

9. Rest 5 to 10 minutes so the dish can settle, then serve. Rest means let cooked meat sit so the juices settle back into the meat.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The center should be steaming hot, 165 degrees F if it was assembled ahead. You should smell toasted cheese and pasta, not scorched milk. The pasta should feel tender.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The top should look golden and bubbling, with browned spots if you used crumbs or extra cheese. The edges should look set, not burnt.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The top should look golden and bubbling, with browned spots if you used crumbs or extra cheese. The edges should look set, not burnt.

SAFETY
The dish is very hot. Use mitts. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F with a splash of milk if they have dried out. Serve cream and cheese sauces hot. Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F, or until a sauce or gravy returns to a boil.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Cover with foil if the top browns before the center is hot, then uncover for the last few minutes. A mix of cheeses, such as cheddar and mozzarella, melts well.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
