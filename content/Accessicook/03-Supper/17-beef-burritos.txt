Beef Burritos
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Large tortillas rolled around seasoned beef, rice or beans, and cheese, then optionally crisped in a skillet. Cook the beef to 160 degrees F. Do not overfill the tortillas.

SERVINGS
4

TOTAL TIME
About 30 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A large skillet or saute pan
- A heat-safe spatula or wooden spoon
- A splatter screen
- A talking or instant-read food thermometer

INGREDIENTS
- 1 pound ground beef, cooked as in the Tacos recipe, to 160 degrees F
- 4 large flour tortillas
- 1 cup cooked rice, or 1 cup warm beans
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- Salsa, optional
- 1 teaspoon oil, if you are crisping the burritos

TACTILE TIPS
Warm tortillas roll without tearing. Leave space at the ends so you can fold them in. Fold means gently turn the mixture over itself so you do not knock out the air. The center should be hot when you serve.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Cook the beef to 160 degrees F with seasoning, as in Tacos, or reheat leftover taco meat to 165 degrees F.

2. Warm the rice or beans until steaming hot, 165 degrees F if leftover.

3. Warm the tortillas until they feel flexible.

4. Add beef, rice or beans, cheese, and a little salsa down the center of each tortilla.

5. Fold in the ends and roll into a log.

6. If you want a crisp outside, cook in a lightly oiled skillet 2 minutes per side, until the tortilla feels crisp and the center is hot.

7. Serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The filling should be hot. Leftover components should be 165 degrees F. The tortilla should feel flexible, or crisp if browned. You should smell warm beef and toasting tortilla.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The tortilla should look soft, or golden if crisped. Cheese at the ends may look melted.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The tortilla should look soft, or golden if crisped. Cheese at the ends may look melted.

SAFETY
Cook new ground beef to 160 degrees F. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Let a very hot center cool a moment before the first bite. Cook ground beef to 160 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
These freeze well after they cool. Reheat from frozen, wrapped in foil, in a 350 degree F oven until the center is 165 degrees F.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
