Ground Turkey Skillet
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A one-pan supper of ground turkey, vegetables, and a simple seasoning or sauce, served over rice or in tortillas. The turkey must reach 165 degrees F.

SERVINGS
4

TOTAL TIME
About 30 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A large skillet or saute pan
- A heat-safe spatula or wooden spoon
- A splatter screen
- A talking or instant-read food thermometer

INGREDIENTS
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1 pound ground turkey
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced, or 2 cups other cut vegetables
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon chili powder or Italian seasoning
- 1 can (14 1/2 ounces) diced tomatoes, or 1/2 cup (half a cup) salsa
- Optional: 1 cup shredded cheese, or cooked rice, for serving

TACTILE TIPS
Ground turkey feels sticky and pale when raw. It should turn firm and crumbly as it cooks, and a thermometer should read 165 degrees F in several places, because a skillet of ground meat does not always cook evenly.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat.

2. Add the onion and pepper.

3. Cook 4 to 5 minutes, until they feel soft.

4. Add the turkey, salt, and seasoning.

5. Cook, breaking it up, until it is 165 degrees F and no longer pasty and raw, about 8 minutes.

6. Add the garlic.

7. Cook 30 seconds.

8. Add the tomatoes or salsa.

9. Simmer 5 minutes. Simmer means small, gentle bubbles.

10. Taste a cooled bite and add salt if it needs it.

11. Add cheese if you like, then serve with rice or tortillas.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: A thermometer must read 165 degrees F in several spots. The mix should smell savory, like onion and cooked turkey, not raw meat.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The turkey should be opaque and browned in spots, with no raw pink. The vegetables should look soft. The mix should look moist, not dry and gray, and not watery.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The turkey should be opaque and browned in spots, with no raw pink. The vegetables should look soft. The mix should look moist, not dry and gray, and not watery.

SAFETY
Wash hands after handling raw ground turkey. Cook to 165 degrees F, not 160. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Cook ground chicken or ground turkey to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
This is a good base for taco night or a rice bowl. Drain extra liquid if the tomatoes make it soupy, or simmer a few minutes more. Drain means pour off the liquid.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
