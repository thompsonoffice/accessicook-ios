ACCESSICOOK
Taco Skillet
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A one-pan supper of taco-seasoned beef, rice or beans, corn, and cheese, served from the skillet. Cook the beef to 160 degrees F. This is a good night when you do not want to assemble individual tacos.

SERVINGS
4

TOTAL TIME
About 30 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A large skillet or saute pan
- A heat-safe spatula or wooden spoon
- A splatter screen
- A talking or instant-read food thermometer

INGREDIENTS
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 packet taco seasoning, or 1 tablespoon chili powder plus 1 teaspoon cumin and 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 can (14 1/2 ounces) diced tomatoes
- 1 cup frozen corn, or 1 can beans, drained
- 1 cup cooked rice, optional
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- Tortilla chips or tortillas, for serving, optional

TACTILE TIPS
The mix should feel moist and scoopable, like a thick skillet hash, not dry and not soupy.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Cook the onion and beef over medium heat until the beef is 160 degrees F.

2. Stir in the seasoning, tomatoes, corn or beans, and optional rice.

3. Simmer 5 to 8 minutes, until everything is hot and slightly thickened. Simmer means cook just below a boil, with small, gentle bubbles.

4. Sprinkle the cheese over the top.

5. Cover 1 minute, until it feels melted.

6. Serve from the skillet, which will be very hot, with chips or tortillas if you like.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: Beef must be 160 degrees F. The skillet should smell like chili and tomato. The cheese should feel melted. Leftover rice in the pan should be steaming hot, 165 degrees F.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The beef should be brown, with no pink. The cheese should look melted. The mix should look thick and chunky, not watery.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The beef should be brown, with no pink. The cheese should look melted. The mix should look thick and chunky, not watery.

SAFETY
Wash hands after raw beef. The skillet handle and lid will be hot. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Cook ground beef to 160 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
This is a good use for leftover rice. A squeeze of lime at the table helps. For a turkey version, cook ground turkey to 165 degrees F.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
