Black Bean Tacos
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A meatless taco supper of seasoned black beans in warm tortillas with toppings. Warm the beans until they are steaming hot, 165 degrees F if they were leftover cooked beans.

SERVINGS
4

TOTAL TIME
About 20 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A large skillet or saute pan
- A heat-safe spatula or wooden spoon
- A splatter screen

INGREDIENTS
- 2 cans (15 ounces each) black beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 tablespoon oil
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) salt
- 1/4 cup (a quarter cup) water or salsa
- 8 tortillas
- Toppings: cheese, lettuce, tomato, or sour cream

TACTILE TIPS
The beans should feel hot and slightly creamy, with some still whole. If you mash a few in the pan, the filling holds together better in a taco.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat.

2. Add the beans, chili powder, cumin, and salt.

3. Add the water or salsa.

4. Cook, stirring and mashing some of the beans, 5 to 8 minutes, until the mix is hot and slightly thick, 165 degrees F if leftover.

5. Warm the tortillas until they feel flexible.

6. Serve the beans in a bowl and let people build their own tacos.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The beans should be steaming hot. You should smell cumin and chili. The mix should feel scoopable and thick enough to stay in a tortilla.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The beans should look moist and a little thickened, not dry and not watery. The tortillas should look soft.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The beans should look moist and a little thickened, not dry and not watery. The tortillas should look soft.

SAFETY
Reheat leftover beans to 165 degrees F. Keep cold toppings cold until serving. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
A squeeze of lime is almost required at the table. Leftover filling works in the Rice and Bean Bowl from the Lunch chapter.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
