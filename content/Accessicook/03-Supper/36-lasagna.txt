ACCESSICOOK
Lasagna
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Layers of pasta, meat or tomato sauce, and cheese baked until the center is hot and the pasta is tender. Cook ground beef to 160 degrees F before you layer. This is a bigger project than baked ziti, but the method is just layering and baking.

SERVINGS
8

TOTAL TIME
About 1 hour 30 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Intermediate

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans
- A talking or instant-read food thermometer

INGREDIENTS
- 12 lasagna noodles, regular or no-boil
- 1 pound ground beef or Italian sausage, optional
- 4 cups marinara sauce
- 15 ounces ricotta cheese, or 2 cups cottage cheese
- 1 large egg
- 3 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, divided
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 teaspoon salt, for the cheese mix, plus more for the pasta water if you boil noodles

TACTILE TIPS
A no-boil noodle feels hard and ruffled and will soften in the sauce, so the layers should be saucy enough. Regular noodles should be cooked until flexible but still firm, then drained. The finished center should feel hot and set, not soupy and not dry.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oven to 375 degrees F.

2. Grease the baking dish if the ingredients include butter or oil for the dish.

3. Cook any meat to 160 degrees F, then stir it into the marinara.

4. Mix the ricotta, egg, 1 cup mozzarella, and a pinch of salt. A pinch is a small amount you can hold between two fingers, about 1/8 teaspoon (an eighth of a teaspoon).

5. In a 9 by 13 inch dish, layer sauce, noodles, ricotta mix, and mozzarella, repeating, and finish with sauce and the remaining cheeses.

6. Bake 45 to 55 minutes, covering with foil for the first 30 minutes if the top browns too fast.

7. The lasagna should be bubbling at the edges, the center should be 165 degrees F or steaming hot, and a knife should slide through the pasta easily.

8. Rest 5 to 10 minutes so the dish can settle, then serve. Rest means let cooked meat sit so the juices settle back into the meat.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: Meat, if used, must be 160 degrees F before layering. After baking, the center should be steaming hot, 165 degrees F, and the pasta should feel tender. You should smell tomato, cheese, and baked pasta. Resting helps it slice.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The top should look bubbly and lightly browned. A knife or spatula in the center should come out with melted cheese and tender pasta, not hard, chalky noodles, and not a flood of watery sauce.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The top should look bubbly and lightly browned. A knife or spatula in the center should come out with melted cheese and tender pasta, not hard, chalky noodles, and not a flood of watery sauce.

SAFETY
Wash hands after raw meat and raw egg in the ricotta mix. The dish is very hot and heavy. Use mitts and a stable landing zone. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Cook ground beef to 160 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Rest 15 minutes before you cut, or the slices will slump. A vegetarian version can skip the meat and add cooked spinach that has been squeezed dry. No-boil noodles save a pot.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
