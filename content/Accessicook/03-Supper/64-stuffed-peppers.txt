ACCESSICOOK
Stuffed Peppers
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Bell peppers filled with a mix of cooked ground beef, rice, and tomato, then baked until the peppers are tender and the filling is hot. Cook the beef to 160 degrees F before you stuff the peppers.

SERVINGS
4

TOTAL TIME
About 1 hour

DIFFICULTY
Beginner to intermediate

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans
- A talking or instant-read food thermometer

INGREDIENTS
- 4 bell peppers, tops cut off and seeds removed
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 cup cooked rice
- 1 cup tomato sauce, divided
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) shredded cheese, optional

TACTILE TIPS
Choose peppers that can stand up in a dish. A thin slice off the bottom, without cutting through, can help a wobbly pepper sit flat. The filling should be cooked and only needs to heat through with the peppers.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oven to 375 degrees F.

2. Grease the baking dish if the ingredients include butter or oil for the dish.

3. Cook the onion and beef until the beef is 160 degrees F.

4. Stir in the rice, 2/3 of the tomato sauce, salt, and seasoning.

5. Stand the hollowed peppers in a baking dish.

6. Fill them.

7. Spoon the remaining sauce over the tops.

8. Add cheese in the last 10 minutes if you like.

9. Bake 30 to 40 minutes.

10. The peppers should feel tender, and the filling should be steaming hot.

11. Rest 5 to 10 minutes so the dish can settle, then serve. Rest means let cooked meat sit so the juices settle back into the meat.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The beef must be 160 degrees F before stuffing. The baked peppers should feel tender when pierced. The filling should be steaming hot, 165 degrees F if it was assembled ahead and chilled. You should smell roasted pepper, tomato, and beef.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The peppers should look slightly wrinkled and duller, not raw and tight. The filling should look hot, and the cheese, if used, should look melted. The beef in the filling should already have been browned, with no raw look.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The peppers should look slightly wrinkled and duller, not raw and tight. The filling should look hot, and the cheese, if used, should look melted. The beef in the filling should already have been browned, with no raw look.

SAFETY
Wash hands after raw beef. The baking dish is hot. Stand the peppers in a dish with a little water or extra sauce so they do not scorch. Cook ground beef to 160 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Turkey can replace beef, but cook it to 165 degrees F before stuffing. Vegetarian versions can use beans and rice and skip the meat.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
