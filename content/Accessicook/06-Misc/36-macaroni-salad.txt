ACCESSICOOK
Macaroni Salad
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Cooked short pasta mixed with a creamy dressing and crisp vegetables, served cold. Cook the pasta until it is tender, then rinse it with cold water so it stops cooking and cools quickly.

SERVINGS
6 as a side

TOTAL TIME
About 30 minutes, plus chilling

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A large pot with a lid
- A wooden spoon or ladle

INGREDIENTS
- 8 ounces elbow macaroni, or another short pasta
- 1 tablespoon salt, for the pasta water
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon vinegar or pickle juice
- 1 teaspoon sugar, optional
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) salt, for the dressing, plus more to taste
- 1 celery stalk, diced
- 1/4 cup (a quarter cup) diced onion or green onion
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) diced bell pepper or shredded carrot, optional

TACTILE TIPS
The pasta should feel tender, not hard in the center, and not mushy. After dressing and chilling, the salad should feel creamy and cool, with a little give in the pasta, not dry and not soupy.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water until it is tender.

2. See the Pasta Primer in the Supper chapter for the basic method.

3. Drain, then rinse under cold water until the pasta feels cool. Drain means pour off the liquid.

4. Drain very well.

5. Mix the mayonnaise, vinegar or pickle juice, optional sugar, and 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) salt in a large bowl.

6. Add the pasta and the vegetables.

7. Toss until evenly coated.

8. Taste a piece, and add salt or vinegar if it needs it.

9. Chill at least 30 minutes before serving.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The pasta should feel tender and cool. You should smell mayonnaise, vinegar, and pasta, not a sour, off smell. The vegetables should feel crisp.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The salad should look creamy and evenly mixed, not dry, and not sitting in a puddle of dressing.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The salad should look creamy and evenly mixed, not dry, and not sitting in a puddle of dressing.

SAFETY
Boiling water can burn you. Cool and refrigerate the salad within 2 hours. Because of mayonnaise, keep it cold, and do not leave it out more than 2 hours, or more than 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Eat within 3 to 4 days. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
If the salad dries out in the refrigerator, fold in a spoon of mayonnaise before serving. Fold means gently turn the mixture over itself so you do not knock out the air. A little mustard in the dressing is a good extra.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
