Ranch Dip
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A cold, creamy dip with herbs, good for raw vegetables, oven fries, and wings. You can use a mix of mayonnaise and sour cream or yogurt. The flavor improves if the dip sits in the refrigerator for 30 minutes so the dried herbs soften.

SERVINGS
About 1 cup, 6 to 8 snack servings

TOTAL TIME
About 10 minutes, plus an optional 30-minute rest

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) mayonnaise
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) sour cream or plain yogurt
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) onion powder
- 1 teaspoon dried parsley, or 1 tablespoon chopped fresh parsley
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) dried dill, optional
- 1/4 teaspoon (a quarter teaspoon) salt, plus more to taste
- 1 tablespoon milk, if the dip is too thick

TACTILE TIPS
The dip should feel smooth and thick enough to cling to a carrot stick, not watery, and not pasty. Dried herbs will feel slightly rough at first and will soften as the dip rests.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Mix the mayonnaise, sour cream or yogurt, garlic powder, onion powder, parsley, optional dill, and salt in a bowl until even.

2. If the dip is too thick, add milk 1 teaspoon at a time.

3. Taste a little, and add salt if it needs it.

4. Cover and refrigerate 30 minutes if you have time, then stir and serve cold, with vegetables or chips.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The dip should feel smooth and thick enough to cling to a vegetable. You should smell garlic, herbs, and a fresh dairy smell, not sourness in an off way. If the sour cream or yogurt smells or looks wrong before you mix, throw it out.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The dip should look smooth and pale, evenly flecked with herbs, not watery and not separated.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The dip should look smooth and pale, evenly flecked with herbs, not watery and not separated.

SAFETY
Keep the dip refrigerated. Do not leave it out more than 2 hours, or more than 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use within 4 days. Provide a spoon for serving if people will be sharing the bowl. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Fresh chives are a good extra. This is the usual partner for Boneless Wings in this chapter and for Oven Fries in the Midnight Snacks chapter.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
