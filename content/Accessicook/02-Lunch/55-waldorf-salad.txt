Waldorf Salad
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A classic mix of apple, celery, grapes, and walnuts in a creamy dressing. It is sweet-savory and good for lunch with bread, or as a side. If anyone has a nut allergy, skip the walnuts or the whole recipe.

SERVINGS
4

TOTAL TIME
About 20 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 2 apples, cored and diced
- 1 cup sliced celery
- 1 cup halved grapes
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) chopped walnuts, optional
- 1/3 cup (a third of a cup) mayonnaise, or half mayonnaise and half yogurt
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice
- A pinch of salt

TACTILE TIPS
Dice the apples just before mixing so they do not brown as much. Dice means cut into small, even cubes. The salad should feel creamy and chunky, with crisp fruit and celery.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Mix the mayonnaise or yogurt with the lemon juice and a pinch of salt. A pinch is a small amount you can hold between two fingers, about 1/8 teaspoon (an eighth of a teaspoon).

2. Add the apples, celery, grapes, and walnuts if you are using them.

3. Toss until coated.

4. Serve, or chill for 30 minutes.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The fruit and celery should feel crisp. The dressing should smell fresh, not sour in a sharp way. Walnuts should smell sweet and nutty, not paint-like or rancid.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The apples should look pale and fresh, not dark brown all over. The dressing should look even and creamy.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The apples should look pale and fresh, not dark brown all over. The dressing should look even and creamy.

SAFETY
Wash the produce. If anyone has a nut allergy, omit the walnuts and avoid cross-contact. Refrigerate leftovers and eat within 2 days. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
A little honey in the dressing makes it sweeter. Toast the walnuts in a dry skillet until they smell nutty, then cool them, for more flavor.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
