Hummus Veggie Wrap
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A tortilla spread with hummus and filled with crisp vegetables. It packs well for lunch if you dry the vegetables first.

SERVINGS
1

TOTAL TIME
About 15 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food

INGREDIENTS
- 1 large flour tortilla
- 1/3 cup (a third of a cup) hummus
- Sliced cucumber, carrot, bell pepper, and lettuce
- A pinch of salt

TACTILE TIPS
Do not overfill. A tight roll holds better than a stuffed one that bursts. Dry the vegetables.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Wash, slice, and pat dry the vegetables.

2. Warm the tortilla until it feels flexible.

3. Lay the bread, roll, or wrap on the board.

4. Spread the hummus.

5. Add the vegetables and a pinch of salt. A pinch is a small amount you can hold between two fingers, about 1/8 teaspoon (an eighth of a teaspoon).

6. Fold the sides and roll it up. Fold means gently turn the mixture over itself so you do not knock out the air.

7. Cut in half if you like. Cut in means rub or cut cold butter into flour until the mix feels like crumbs.

8. Serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The wrap should feel tight. The vegetables should feel crisp. The hummus should smell like sesame and chickpea, not sour.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The vegetables should look fresh and crisp. The tortilla should look soft, with no dry cracks.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The vegetables should look fresh and crisp. The tortilla should look soft, with no dry cracks.

SAFETY
Wash the vegetables. Keep the wrap cold if you pack it. Eat the same day for the best texture. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
See Hummus in the Misc chapter. A handful of feta or leftover chicken, if reheated or kept cold properly, can make it more filling.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
