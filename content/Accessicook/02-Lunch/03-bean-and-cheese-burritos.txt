Bean and Cheese Burritos
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Warm tortillas filled with refried or whole beans and cheese, then rolled and optionally crisped in a skillet. This is a fast meatless lunch.

SERVINGS
4

TOTAL TIME
About 20 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A large skillet or saute pan
- A heat-safe spatula or wooden spoon
- A splatter screen

INGREDIENTS
- 4 large flour tortillas
- 1 can (15 ounces) refried beans, or 1 1/2 cups (one and a half cups) cooked beans, lightly mashed
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- Salsa, optional
- 1 teaspoon oil, if you are crisping the burritos

TACTILE TIPS
Warm tortillas roll without cracking. Do not overfill. The ends should tuck in so the filling stays put.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Warm the beans in a pan or microwave until they are steaming hot, 165 degrees F if they were leftover.

2. Warm the tortillas until they feel flexible.

3. Spoon beans and cheese down the center of each tortilla.

4. Add salsa if you want it inside, but a little goes a long way.

5. Fold in the sides and roll into a log. Fold means gently turn the mixture over itself so you do not knock out the air.

6. If you want a crisp outside, cook each burrito in a lightly oiled skillet over medium heat 2 minutes per side, until the tortilla feels crisp and the center is hot.

7. Serve with more salsa if you like.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The filling should be hot. The tortilla should feel flexible, or crisp if you browned it. You should smell warm beans and toasting tortilla.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The tortilla should look soft, or golden if you crisped it. Cheese may show at the ends and should look melted if the burrito was heated.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The tortilla should look soft, or golden if you crisped it. Cheese may show at the ends and should look melted if the burrito was heated.

SAFETY
Reheat leftover beans to 165 degrees F. The skillet is hot if you are crisping. Let a very hot center cool a moment so no one burns their mouth. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Leftover rice can go in the filling. Wrap extras in foil and refrigerate. Reheat to 165 degrees F in the oven or a skillet.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
