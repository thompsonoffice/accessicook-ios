ACCESSICOOK
Club Sandwich
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A stacked sandwich with toast, cooked chicken or turkey, bacon, lettuce, tomato, and mayonnaise. It is often made with three slices of toast. All meats should already be cooked.

SERVINGS
1

TOTAL TIME
About 25 minutes, if the chicken and bacon are already cooked

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food

INGREDIENTS
- 3 slices bread, toasted
- 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 3 or 4 slices cooked chicken or turkey
- 2 slices cooked bacon
- Lettuce and tomato slices

TACTILE TIPS
Keep fillings in even layers so the stack does not slide. Toothpicks can hold it together if you have them, but warn anyone eating it.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Toast the bread.

2. Make sure the chicken or turkey and the bacon are already cooked.

3. Lay the bread, roll, or wrap on the board.

4. Spread mayonnaise on the toast.

5. Build two layers: toast, lettuce, tomato, poultry, bacon, toast, then the remaining fillings, then the last toast.

6. Cut in half if you like. Cut in means rub or cut cold butter into flour until the mix feels like crumbs.

7. Serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The sandwich should feel sturdy and the meats should be cold or fully reheated, never lukewarm for a long time on the counter. Cooked leftovers used hot should be 165 degrees F.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The toast should be golden. Meats should look cooked, not raw. Lettuce should look fresh.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The toast should be golden. Meats should look cooked, not raw. Lettuce should look fresh.

SAFETY
Use already-cooked poultry and bacon. Wash produce. If you reheat the chicken, it must reach 165 degrees F. Refrigerate leftovers promptly. Cook all poultry to 165 degrees F. Measure in the thickest part. Do not let the thermometer tip touch bone or the pan.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Cut the sandwich in quarters if it is tall. A toothpick in each quarter helps, but remove them before a child eats.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
