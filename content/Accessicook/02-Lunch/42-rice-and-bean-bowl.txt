ACCESSICOOK
Rice and Bean Bowl
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A simple lunch bowl of rice, beans, salsa, and toppings. Use leftover rice that you reheat until steaming, or cook a fresh pot using the Stovetop Rice method in the Misc chapter.

SERVINGS
4

TOTAL TIME
About 35 minutes, or less with leftover rice

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A large pot with a lid
- A wooden spoon or ladle

INGREDIENTS
- 3 cups cooked rice, or 1 cup dry rice, cooked
- 1 can (15 ounces) black beans or pinto beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup salsa
- 1 cup shredded cheese, optional
- Optional toppings: lettuce, sour cream, or avocado

TACTILE TIPS
Reheated rice should feel hot and fluffy, not hard and cold in the center, and not dried into a crust unless you like a little crispness.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Cook the rice, or reheat leftover rice with a splash of water until it is steaming hot, 165 degrees F.

2. Warm the beans until they are steaming hot, 165 degrees F if leftover.

3. Divide the rice among bowls.

4. Add beans, salsa, cheese, and any toppings.

5. Serve at once.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The rice and beans should be steaming hot, 165 degrees F for leftovers. Salsa should smell fresh and tomatoey, not fermented in an off way if it has been open a long time.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The rice should look fluffy and steaming. The beans should look hot and moist, not dried out.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The rice should look fluffy and steaming. The beans should look hot and moist, not dried out.

SAFETY
Leftover rice should be cooled quickly when first cooked, then reheated until steaming hot, 165 degrees F. Do not leave cooked rice out for hours at room temperature. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Leftover taco meat or roasted vegetables can go in the bowl. A squeeze of lime helps. See Stovetop Rice in the Misc chapter for the basic rice method.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
