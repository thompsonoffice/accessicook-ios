Broccoli Salad
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A crunchy, creamy salad of raw broccoli florets with a slightly sweet dressing, often mixed with onion, cheese, and optional bacon or seeds.

SERVINGS
6

TOTAL TIME
About 20 minutes, plus 30 minutes of chilling

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 5 cups small broccoli florets
- 1/4 cup (a quarter cup) diced red onion
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) shredded cheddar, optional
- 1/3 cup (a third of a cup) mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon vinegar
- 1 tablespoon sugar, or to taste
- 1/4 teaspoon (a quarter teaspoon) salt
- Optional: 4 slices cooked bacon, crumbled, or 1/4 cup sunflower seeds

TACTILE TIPS
Cut the florets small so they are easy to eat raw. The salad should feel crunchy and evenly coated, not pasty with too much dressing.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Cut the broccoli into small florets.

2. Wash and dry them well.

3. Mix the mayonnaise, vinegar, sugar, and salt until smooth.

4. Toss the broccoli, onion, cheese, and any bacon or seeds with the dressing.

5. Chill at least 30 minutes.

6. Toss again and serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The broccoli should feel crisp and raw-crunchy, not rubbery or off-smelling. The dressing should taste balanced, not harsh with raw onion until after the chill.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The broccoli should look bright green and coated, not gray or yellow. Bacon, if used, should look cooked.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The broccoli should look bright green and coated, not gray or yellow. Bacon, if used, should look cooked.

SAFETY
Wash the broccoli. Cook bacon fully if you use it. Refrigerate and eat within 3 days. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
If raw onion is too sharp, soak the diced onion in cold water 10 minutes, then drain and dry it. Dice means cut into small, even cubes. Drain means pour off the liquid. You can blanch the broccoli 1 minute and cool it if you want it less raw. Blanch means cook briefly in boiling water, then cool.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
