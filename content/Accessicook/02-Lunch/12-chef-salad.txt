ACCESSICOOK
Chef Salad
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A large green salad topped with strips of cheese and already-cooked meats, such as turkey, ham, and hard-boiled egg. It is a composed lunch salad, meaning you arrange the toppings rather than tossing everything into a mush.

SERVINGS
2

TOTAL TIME
About 20 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 4 cups salad greens, washed and dried
- 2 ounces sliced turkey
- 2 ounces sliced cooked ham
- 2 ounces cheese, cut into strips
- 2 hard-boiled eggs, quartered
- Tomato wedges or cucumber slices
- 1/4 cup (a quarter cup) dressing of your choice

TACTILE TIPS
Keep the toppings in loose piles on the greens so you can choose a little of each in a bite. Dry the greens.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Wash and dry the greens and vegetables.

2. Cook and peel the eggs if you have not already, using the Hard-Boiled Eggs recipe.

3. Cut the meats, cheese, and eggs.

4. Put the salad base and the mix-ins in a large bowl.

5. Arrange the greens in bowls.

6. Lay the meats, cheese, egg, and vegetables on top.

7. Serve the dressing on the side, or drizzle it on now.

8. Taste a leaf and add a pinch of salt if it needs it. A pinch is a small amount you can hold between two fingers, about 1/8 teaspoon (an eighth of a teaspoon).

9. Serve soon, while the greens are crisp.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: Greens should feel crisp. Meats should feel cool and smell fresh. Eggs should feel fully set. Dressing can be served on the side so the salad does not wilt.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The plate should look neat, with visible strips of meat, cheese, and egg on a bed of greens. Meats should look cooked. Eggs should look fully set, with dry, pale yolks.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The plate should look neat, with visible strips of meat, cheese, and egg on a bed of greens. Meats should look cooked. Eggs should look fully set, with dry, pale yolks.

SAFETY
Use already-cooked meats and fully cooked eggs. Keep the salad cold. Wash produce. Eat soon, or refrigerate and eat the same day.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Serve the dressing on the side. This is a good use for leftover ham, turkey, and hard-boiled eggs from other chapters.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
