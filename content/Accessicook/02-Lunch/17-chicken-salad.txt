ACCESSICOOK
Chicken Salad
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Chopped cooked chicken mixed with mayonnaise and crunchy celery or fruit. Use leftover roast chicken or poached chicken that was cooked to 165 degrees F.

SERVINGS
3 to 4

TOTAL TIME
About 20 minutes, if the chicken is already cooked

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 2 cups chopped cooked chicken
- 1/3 cup (a third of a cup) mayonnaise
- 1 celery stalk, diced
- 1 teaspoon lemon juice or mustard
- Salt and pepper
- Optional: 1/3 cup halved grapes or chopped apple

TACTILE TIPS
The chicken should be fully cooked and cool enough to handle. The salad should feel moist and chunky, not pasty.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Make sure the chicken was cooked to 165 degrees F, then cool it and chop it.

2. Mix the chicken, mayonnaise, celery, lemon or mustard, and any fruit.

3. Add salt and pepper to taste.

4. Chill if you have time, then serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The chicken should feel firm and cooked, not pasty and raw. The salad should smell clean and savory, not sour.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The chicken should look opaque and cooked, with no pink, raw look. The salad should look evenly dressed.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The chicken should look opaque and cooked, with no pink, raw look. The salad should look evenly dressed.

SAFETY
Start with chicken that reached 165 degrees F. Refrigerate the salad promptly. Eat within 3 to 4 days. Cook all poultry to 165 degrees F. Measure in the thickest part. Do not let the thermometer tip touch bone or the pan.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Use rotisserie chicken to save time, removing the meat from the bones. Toasted pecans add crunch if no one has a nut allergy.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
