ACCESSICOOK
Greek Salad
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A chunky salad of tomato, cucumber, onion, olives, and feta cheese with an oil and lemon or vinegar dressing. It is hearty enough for lunch without lettuce, though you can add some.

SERVINGS
2 to 3

TOTAL TIME
About 20 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 2 tomatoes, cut into chunks
- 1 cucumber, sliced
- 1/4 of a red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) pitted olives
- 4 ounces feta cheese, cubed or crumbled
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice or red wine vinegar
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) dried oregano
- Salt and pepper

TACTILE TIPS
Cut the vegetables into large, even chunks you can spear with a fork. Feta feels crumbly and salty. Olives should be pitted so no one bites a stone.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Wash and cut the vegetables.

2. Pit the olives if needed.

3. Whisk the oil, lemon or vinegar, oregano, and a pinch of pepper. A pinch is a small amount you can hold between two fingers, about 1/8 teaspoon (an eighth of a teaspoon). Whisk means beat with a fork or a whisk until the mixture looks even.

4. Put the salad base and the mix-ins in a large bowl.

5. Combine the vegetables, olives, and feta.

6. Pour the dressing over and toss gently.

7. Taste a leaf and add a pinch of salt if it needs it.

8. Serve soon, while the greens are crisp.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The vegetables should feel crisp-tender and smell fresh. The feta should smell clean and milky-salty, not sour. Watch for olive pits if the label is unclear.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The vegetables should look juicy and fresh. The dressing should look glossy on the surface, not pooled as a large puddle of oil.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The vegetables should look juicy and fresh. The dressing should look glossy on the surface, not pooled as a large puddle of oil.

SAFETY
Wash the vegetables. Pit olives if they are not already pitted. Keep feta cold until you mix. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Add a pinch of salt only after you taste, because feta and olives are already salty. Serve with pita if you like.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
