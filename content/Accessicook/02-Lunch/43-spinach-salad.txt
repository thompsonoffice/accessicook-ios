ACCESSICOOK
Spinach Salad
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Fresh spinach with a simple dressing and optional warm toppings such as mushrooms or bacon. If you add bacon, cook it fully first.

SERVINGS
2

TOTAL TIME
About 15 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 5 ounces fresh spinach, washed and dried, tough stems removed
- 1/4 cup (a quarter cup) vinaigrette
- Optional: 2 hard-boiled eggs, sliced, or 4 slices cooked bacon, crumbled
- Optional: 1/2 cup (half a cup) sliced mushrooms or toasted nuts

TACTILE TIPS
Dry the spinach well. Tender leaves feel thin and slightly slippery. Discard any slimy or foul-smelling leaves.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Wash and dry the spinach.

2. Prepare any toppings, cooking bacon or eggs fully if you are using them.

3. Put the salad base and the mix-ins in a large bowl.

4. Toss the spinach with the vinaigrette.

5. Add toppings and serve at once.

6. Taste a leaf and add a pinch of salt if it needs it. A pinch is a small amount you can hold between two fingers, about 1/8 teaspoon (an eighth of a teaspoon).

7. Serve soon, while the greens are crisp.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The leaves should feel dry and crisp-tender. They should smell fresh and grassy, not sour or swampy.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The spinach should look dark green and lively, not yellow or slimy. Optional bacon should look cooked. Egg yolks should look set.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The spinach should look dark green and lively, not yellow or slimy. Optional bacon should look cooked. Egg yolks should look set.

SAFETY
Wash spinach carefully, because grit hides in the stems. Use fully cooked eggs or bacon if you add them. If anyone has a nut allergy, skip nuts. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Dress the salad just before serving. Warm bacon or mushrooms can be added at the last minute to wilt the spinach slightly, which some people like.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
