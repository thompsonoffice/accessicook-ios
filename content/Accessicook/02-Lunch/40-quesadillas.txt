ACCESSICOOK
Quesadillas
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A tortilla folded over melted cheese and optional fillings, cooked in a skillet until the outside is crisp. This is the lunch version. Keep fillings already cooked and not too wet.

SERVINGS
2

TOTAL TIME
About 15 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A large skillet or saute pan
- A heat-safe spatula or wooden spoon
- A splatter screen

INGREDIENTS
- 2 large flour tortillas
- 1 1/2 cups (one and a half cups) shredded cheese
- Optional fillings: leftover cooked chicken, beans, or sautéed peppers, already cooked and hot or fully reheated
- 1 teaspoon oil or a little butter, for the pan
- Salsa or sour cream, for serving

TACTILE TIPS
Do not overfill. Cheese should be close to the fold so it melts and acts like glue. Fold means gently turn the mixture over itself so you do not knock out the air. The finished quesadilla should feel crisp outside and soft and melted inside.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat a skillet over medium heat and add a little oil or butter.

2. Lay a tortilla in the pan.

3. Sprinkle cheese on one half.

4. Add any cooked fillings.

5. Add a little more cheese.

6. Fold the tortilla over.

7. Cook 2 to 4 minutes per side, until the tortilla feels crisp and the cheese feels fully melted.

8. Cut into wedges.

9. Serve with salsa or sour cream.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The tortilla should feel crisp. The center should feel hot and melted. You should smell toasting tortilla and cheese.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The outside should be golden with toasted spots. Cheese should look melted, not solid and pale.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The outside should be golden with toasted spots. Cheese should look melted, not solid and pale.

SAFETY
Any leftover meat filling must be reheated to 165 degrees F before or as it goes in. Use a spatula to turn the quesadilla so it does not unfold. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
A breakfast version is in the Breakfast chapter, with eggs. Keep wet salsa on the side, not inside, so the tortilla stays crisp.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
