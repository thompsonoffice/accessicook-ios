ACCESSICOOK
Fish Sandwich
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Baked breaded fish fillets on toasted buns with tartar sauce and optional lettuce. This is an oven method, which is easier than frying. Cook the fish to 145 degrees F, or until it flakes.

SERVINGS
2

TOTAL TIME
About 30 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans
- A talking or instant-read food thermometer

INGREDIENTS
- 2 breaded fish fillets, homemade or frozen
- 2 buns
- 2 tablespoons tartar sauce or mayonnaise
- Lettuce or a slice of cheese, optional

TACTILE TIPS
The fish should feel opaque and flaky in the center when done, not translucent and wet. Frozen fillets should be cooked from frozen unless the package says otherwise.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Bake the fish according to the package, or at 425 degrees F for about 15 to 20 minutes, until a thermometer in the center reads 145 degrees F or the flesh flakes easily with a fork.

2. Toast the buns if you like.

3. Spread tartar sauce on the buns.

4. Add the fish and any lettuce or cheese.

5. Serve at once.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The fish should feel hot, opaque, and flaky, and smell clean and cooked, not strongly fishy in a sour way. A thermometer should read 145 degrees F.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The coating should be golden. The fish should look opaque, not glassy and raw, and it should flake when you press it with a fork.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The coating should be golden. The fish should look opaque, not glassy and raw, and it should flake when you press it with a fork.

SAFETY
Follow package directions for frozen fish if they differ slightly, but still confirm 145 degrees F. The pan is hot. Serve soon after baking. Cook fish to 145 degrees F, or until the flesh is opaque and flakes, meaning it comes apart easily with a fork.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
A squeeze of lemon helps. For homemade breaded fish, see Baked White Fish in the Supper chapter, then put it on a bun.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
