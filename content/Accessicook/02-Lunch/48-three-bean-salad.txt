ACCESSICOOK
Three Bean Salad
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A pantry salad of canned beans with a sweet-tart vinaigrette. It keeps well in the refrigerator and is useful for lunches you pack ahead.

SERVINGS
6

TOTAL TIME
About 15 minutes, plus at least 30 minutes of chilling

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 1 can (15 ounces) kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 can (15 ounces) green beans, drained, or 2 cups cooked green beans
- 1 can (15 ounces) chickpeas or wax beans, drained and rinsed
- 1/4 cup (a quarter cup) diced onion
- 1/4 cup oil
- 1/4 cup vinegar
- 2 tablespoons sugar, or to taste
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) salt
- 1/4 teaspoon (a quarter teaspoon) black pepper

TACTILE TIPS
Rinse canned beans until they feel less slippery and the foamy can liquid is gone. The finished salad should feel chilled, glossy, and not dusty with unrinsed starch.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Drain and rinse the beans. Drain means pour off the liquid.

2. Put them in a bowl with the onion.

3. Mix the oil, vinegar, sugar, salt, and pepper until the sugar feels dissolved.

4. Pour the dressing over the beans.

5. Toss well.

6. Cover and refrigerate at least 30 minutes.

7. Toss again before you serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The salad should taste balanced between sweet, salt, and vinegar. The beans should feel tender. Onion should be present but not harsh after a rest in the dressing. Rest means let cooked meat sit so the juices settle back into the meat.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The beans should look shiny and evenly dressed, not dry and not swimming in a huge puddle of dressing.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The beans should look shiny and evenly dressed, not dry and not swimming in a huge puddle of dressing.

SAFETY
Refrigerate. Eat within 3 to 4 days. Use a clean spoon each time you serve from the bowl. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
This is better the next day. You can add diced bell pepper for crunch. Dice means cut into small, even cubes.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
