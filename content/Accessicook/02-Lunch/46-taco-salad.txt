ACCESSICOOK
Taco Salad
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A bowl of greens, beans, cheese, and seasoned ground beef, with crushed chips or a tortilla for crunch. Cook the beef to 160 degrees F. You can also use leftover taco meat from the Supper chapter, reheated to 165 degrees F.

SERVINGS
4

TOTAL TIME
About 30 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 tablespoon taco seasoning, or 1 teaspoon chili powder plus 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) cumin and 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 6 cups chopped lettuce, washed and dried
- 1 can (15 ounces) black beans or kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 1 cup shredded cheese
- 1 tomato, diced
- 2 cups tortilla chips, lightly crushed
- Salsa or sour cream, optional

TACTILE TIPS
Keep wet toppings and chips separate until you serve so the chips stay crisp. The beef should feel browned and crumbly, not raw and pasty.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Cook the ground beef in a skillet over medium heat, breaking it up, until a thermometer reads 160 degrees F and no raw, pink, pasty meat remains.

2. Stir in the seasoning.

3. Taste a cooled bite and adjust salt.

4. Divide the lettuce among bowls.

5. Add beef, beans, tomato, and cheese.

6. Add crushed chips just before you eat, plus salsa or sour cream if you like.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The beef must be 160 degrees F. The lettuce should feel crisp. The chips should feel crisp when added, not already soggy.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The beef should be brown, with no pink. The lettuce should look crisp. The chips should look dry until they are added.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The beef should be brown, with no pink. The lettuce should look crisp. The chips should look dry until they are added.

SAFETY
Wash hands and the board after raw beef. Reheat leftover taco meat to 165 degrees F. Serve soon after adding chips. Cook ground beef to 160 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
For a vegetarian bowl, skip the beef and add extra beans, or use leftover Vegetarian Chili, reheated to 165 degrees F.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
