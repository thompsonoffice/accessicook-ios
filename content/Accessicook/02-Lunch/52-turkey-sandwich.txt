Turkey Sandwich
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A simple sliced turkey sandwich with your choice of condiment, cheese, and vegetables. Use leftover roast turkey or deli turkey that has been kept cold.

SERVINGS
1

TOTAL TIME
About 10 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food

INGREDIENTS
- 2 slices bread
- 2 to 4 ounces sliced turkey
- 1 teaspoon mustard or mayonnaise
- Optional: cheese, lettuce, or tomato

TACTILE TIPS
Deli turkey is thin and moist. It should smell clean, not sour or slimy.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Lay the bread, roll, or wrap on the board.

2. Spread mustard or mayonnaise on the bread.

3. Add turkey and any cheese or vegetables.

4. Close the sandwich.

5. Cut in half if you like. Cut in means rub or cut cold butter into flour until the mix feels like crumbs.

6. Serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The turkey should feel cool and smell fresh. If it smells sour or feels sticky in a slimy way, throw it out.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The turkey should look moist and even in color, not slimy or gray-green. Lettuce should look fresh.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The turkey should look moist and even in color, not slimy or gray-green. Lettuce should look fresh.

SAFETY
Keep deli meat refrigerated. Do not leave the sandwich out more than 2 hours. Pregnant people and others at higher risk may be advised to heat deli meat until steaming. Cook all poultry to 165 degrees F. Measure in the thickest part. Do not let the thermometer tip touch bone or the pan.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
A spread of mustard and a slice of cheese is a classic combo. Whole-grain bread adds a nutty flavor you can smell.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
