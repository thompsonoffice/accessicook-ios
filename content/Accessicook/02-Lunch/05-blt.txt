ACCESSICOOK
BLT
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A bacon, lettuce, and tomato sandwich on toasted bread with mayonnaise. Cook the bacon fully first, using the Bacon recipe in the Breakfast chapter if you need the method.

SERVINGS
1

TOTAL TIME
About 20 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food

INGREDIENTS
- 2 slices bread
- 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 3 or 4 slices cooked bacon, still warm or at room temperature
- 2 leaves lettuce, washed and dried
- 2 slices tomato
- A pinch of salt and pepper

TACTILE TIPS
Dry the lettuce well so the bread does not get soggy. Tomato slices should feel ripe and hold together. Bacon should feel crisp or tender-crisp, not raw and floppy.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Cook the bacon until it is done, using the Breakfast chapter Bacon recipe, then drain it. Drain means pour off the liquid.

2. Lay the bread, roll, or wrap on the board.

3. Spread mayonnaise on both slices of bread.

4. Add lettuce, tomato, salt, pepper, and bacon.

5. Close the sandwich.

6. Cut in half if you like. Cut in means rub or cut cold butter into flour until the mix feels like crumbs.

7. Serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The toast should feel dry and crisp. The bacon should feel cooked, not raw. The tomato should smell fresh.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The toast should be golden. The bacon should look cooked. The tomato should look ripe, not pale and hard or mushy and leaking.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The toast should be golden. The bacon should look cooked. The tomato should look ripe, not pale and hard or mushy and leaking.

SAFETY
Cook bacon fully before you build the sandwich. See the Bacon recipe for cues and for turkey bacon, which must reach 165 degrees F. Wash the tomato and lettuce. For pork chops, ribs, and roasts, the USDA minimum is 145 degrees F, then a 3-minute rest. For ribs or pulled pork, also cook until the meat is tender.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Toast the bread so it stands up to the tomato juice. Add avocado if you like.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
