Cucumber Tomato Salad
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A quick salad of cucumber and tomato with oil, vinegar or lemon, and salt. It is juicy and good next to a sandwich.

SERVINGS
4

TOTAL TIME
About 15 minutes, plus optional 15 minutes of rest

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 2 cucumbers, sliced
- 2 tomatoes, cut into wedges
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon vinegar or lemon juice
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) salt
- 1/4 teaspoon (a quarter teaspoon) black pepper
- Optional: a few thin slices of onion, or chopped fresh herbs

TACTILE TIPS
If the cucumber has thick, tough skin, you can peel it. The salad will release juice after it sits with salt. That is normal.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Put the cucumber, tomato, and optional onion in a bowl.

2. Add the oil, vinegar or lemon, salt, pepper, and herbs if you are using them.

3. Toss gently.

4. Taste a piece and add salt if it needs it.

5. Let it sit 10 to 15 minutes if you have time, then toss again and serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The cucumber should feel crisp. The tomato should feel ripe. The dressing should smell fresh and sharp from the vinegar or lemon.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The vegetables should look juicy and bright, not dull or slimy.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The vegetables should look juicy and bright, not dull or slimy.

SAFETY
Wash the produce. Refrigerate leftovers and eat within 2 days. The salad will get juicier as it sits. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Salt draws out liquid, so if you need a drier salad, salt just before serving. A pinch of sugar can balance very acidic tomatoes. A pinch is a small amount you can hold between two fingers, about 1/8 teaspoon (an eighth of a teaspoon).

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
