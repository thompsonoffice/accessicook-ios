Caesar Salad
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Romaine lettuce with Caesar dressing, Parmesan, and croutons. This version uses bottled or homemade dressing without raw egg, which is safer than a traditional raw-egg Caesar.

SERVINGS
2

TOTAL TIME
About 15 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 4 cups chopped romaine lettuce, washed and dried
- 1/3 cup (a third of a cup) Caesar dressing, egg-free
- 1/4 cup (a quarter cup) grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 cup croutons

TACTILE TIPS
Dry the romaine well. The dressed leaves should feel lightly coated, not heavy and soggy.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Wash, dry, and chop the romaine.

2. Put the salad base and the mix-ins in a large bowl.

3. Toss the lettuce with the dressing and Parmesan, then add the croutons just before you serve.

4. Taste a leaf and add a pinch of salt if it needs it. A pinch is a small amount you can hold between two fingers, about 1/8 teaspoon (an eighth of a teaspoon).

5. Serve soon, while the greens are crisp.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The lettuce should feel crisp. The salad should smell of garlic, cheese, and lemon or anchovy, depending on the dressing, not sour or off.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The lettuce should look crisp and green. The dressing should look creamy and even. Croutons should look dry and golden, not stale and soft before you add them.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The lettuce should look crisp and green. The dressing should look creamy and even. Croutons should look dry and golden, not stale and soft before you add them.

SAFETY
Use a dressing that does not contain raw egg, or use a pasteurized egg dressing if you prefer a traditional style. Wash the lettuce. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
For Chicken Caesar Salad, see the next recipe. Toss the dressing and cheese first, then add croutons so they stay crisp.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
