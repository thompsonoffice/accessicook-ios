Caprese Salad
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Sliced tomato and fresh mozzarella with basil, oil, and a pinch of salt. A pinch is a small amount you can hold between two fingers, about 1/8 teaspoon (an eighth of a teaspoon). It is a simple lunch or a starter. There is no lettuce.

SERVINGS
2

TOTAL TIME
About 15 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 2 ripe tomatoes, sliced
- 8 ounces fresh mozzarella, sliced
- A handful of basil leaves
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- A pinch of salt
- Optional: a few drops of balsamic vinegar

TACTILE TIPS
Alternate tomato and mozzarella in a circle or a row so each bite can include both. Pat tomato slices if they are very wet.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Slice the tomatoes and mozzarella.

2. Arrange them, overlapping, on a plate.

3. Tuck in basil leaves.

4. Drizzle with oil.

5. Add a pinch of salt, and balsamic if you like.

6. Serve soon.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The tomatoes should smell sweet and fresh. The cheese should smell clean and milky. The oil should smell fruity, not rancid.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The tomatoes should look ripe. The cheese should look fresh and white, not slimy. Basil should look green.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The tomatoes should look ripe. The cheese should look fresh and white, not slimy. Basil should look green.

SAFETY
Wash tomatoes and basil. Keep mozzarella cold until you slice it. Eat the same day. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
This is the salad version of the Caprese Sandwich in this chapter. Ripe in-season tomatoes make the dish. Out of season, cherry tomatoes are often a better choice.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
