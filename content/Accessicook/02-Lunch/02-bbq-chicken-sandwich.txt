BBQ Chicken Sandwich
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Chopped or shredded cooked chicken warmed in barbecue sauce and piled on a bun. Use leftover chicken that was cooked to 165 degrees F.

SERVINGS
2

TOTAL TIME
About 15 minutes, if the chicken is already cooked

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food

INGREDIENTS
- 2 cups shredded cooked chicken
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) barbecue sauce
- 2 buns
- Optional: coleslaw, from the Misc chapter

TACTILE TIPS
The chicken should be moist and hot, and the sauce should coat it without making the bun instantly soggy.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Put the chicken and sauce in a skillet over medium-low heat.

2. Stir until the mix is steaming hot and a thermometer reads 165 degrees F.

3. Toast the buns if you like.

4. Pile the chicken on the buns.

5. Add coleslaw if you like.

6. Serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The filling should be steaming hot and 165 degrees F. You should smell warm barbecue sauce and chicken, not a sour leftover smell.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The chicken should look moist and evenly sauced. The mix should be bubbling slightly at the edges.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The chicken should look moist and evenly sauced. The mix should be bubbling slightly at the edges.

SAFETY
Reheat leftover chicken to 165 degrees F. Do not use chicken that has been in the refrigerator more than 3 to 4 days. Cook all poultry to 165 degrees F. Measure in the thickest part. Do not let the thermometer tip touch bone or the pan.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Pulled pork from the Supper chapter can be used the same way. A sturdy bun holds up better than very soft bread.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
