Veggie Sandwich
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A cold sandwich piled with vegetables, cheese or hummus, and a flavorful spread. Dry the vegetables so the bread does not get soggy.

SERVINGS
1

TOTAL TIME
About 15 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food

INGREDIENTS
- 2 slices bread
- 2 tablespoons hummus, cream cheese, or mayonnaise
- Sliced cucumber, tomato, and bell pepper
- Lettuce or spinach
- Optional: cheese or avocado

TACTILE TIPS
Pat tomato and cucumber dry. Too much moisture makes the bread collapse.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Wash and slice the vegetables.

2. Pat them dry.

3. Lay the bread, roll, or wrap on the board.

4. Spread hummus or another spread on both slices of bread.

5. Layer the vegetables and any cheese or avocado.

6. Close the sandwich.

7. Cut it if you like.

8. Cut in half if you like. Cut in means rub or cut cold butter into flour until the mix feels like crumbs.

9. Serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The vegetables should feel crisp and smell fresh. The spread should help the sandwich hold together.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The vegetables should look fresh and colorful, not wilted or slimy. The bread can be toasted for a drier texture.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The vegetables should look fresh and colorful, not wilted or slimy. The bread can be toasted for a drier texture.

SAFETY
Wash all produce. Keep the sandwich cold if you are packing it. Eat within a few hours if it has mayonnaise, cheese, or avocado. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Toast the bread first. A layer of hummus under the tomato helps block juice. See Hummus in the Misc chapter.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
