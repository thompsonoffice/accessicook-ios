Chicken Caesar Salad
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Caesar salad topped with sliced cooked chicken. Use chicken that was cooked to 165 degrees F, such as leftover roast chicken or the grilled chicken from the sandwich recipe in this chapter.

SERVINGS
2

TOTAL TIME
About 20 minutes, if the chicken is already cooked

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork

INGREDIENTS
- 4 cups chopped romaine lettuce, washed and dried
- 1 1/2 cups (one and a half cups) sliced cooked chicken
- 1/3 cup (a third of a cup) Caesar dressing, egg-free
- 1/4 cup (a quarter cup) grated Parmesan cheese
- 1 cup croutons

TACTILE TIPS
The chicken should be fully cooked and either cold from the refrigerator or reheated to 165 degrees F. Dry the lettuce.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Wash, dry, and chop the romaine.

2. Slice the cooked chicken.

3. Put the salad base and the mix-ins in a large bowl.

4. Toss the lettuce with dressing and Parmesan.

5. Add the chicken and croutons.

6. Taste a leaf and add a pinch of salt if it needs it. A pinch is a small amount you can hold between two fingers, about 1/8 teaspoon (an eighth of a teaspoon).

7. Serve soon, while the greens are crisp.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The chicken should feel firm and cooked. If you serve it hot, it should be 165 degrees F. The lettuce should feel crisp.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The chicken should look opaque and cooked, with no raw pink. The lettuce should look crisp.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The chicken should look opaque and cooked, with no raw pink. The lettuce should look crisp.

SAFETY
Use chicken that reached 165 degrees F. Keep cold chicken cold. Wash the lettuce. Use an egg-free dressing unless you have a safe pasteurized option. Cook all poultry to 165 degrees F. Measure in the thickest part. Do not let the thermometer tip touch bone or the pan.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Warm chicken and cold lettuce is a nice contrast. Slice the chicken just before you toss so it stays moist.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
