Chicken Caesar Wrap
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A tortilla wrapped around cooked chicken, lettuce, Parmesan, and Caesar dressing. Use leftover chicken that reached 165 degrees F.

SERVINGS
1

TOTAL TIME
About 15 minutes, if the chicken is already cooked

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food

INGREDIENTS
- 1 large flour tortilla
- 3/4 cup (three-quarters of a cup) chopped cooked chicken
- 1 cup chopped romaine lettuce, washed and dried
- 2 tablespoons Caesar dressing
- 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese

TACTILE TIPS
Dry the lettuce well. A damp filling makes the wrap soggy and more likely to tear. Warm the tortilla so it bends.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Warm the tortilla until it feels flexible.

2. Mix the chicken, lettuce, dressing, and cheese in a bowl.

3. Lay the bread, roll, or wrap on the board.

4. Spoon the filling down the center of the tortilla.

5. Fold in the sides and roll it up. Fold means gently turn the mixture over itself so you do not knock out the air.

6. Cut in half if you like. Cut in means rub or cut cold butter into flour until the mix feels like crumbs.

7. Serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The chicken should be already cooked and either cold from the fridge or reheated to 165 degrees F. The wrap should feel tight and the lettuce should feel crisp.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The tortilla should look soft, with no dry cracks. The lettuce should look crisp and dry, not wilted and wet.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The tortilla should look soft, with no dry cracks. The lettuce should look crisp and dry, not wilted and wet.

SAFETY
Use chicken that reached 165 degrees F. Keep the wrap cold if you pack it. Wash the lettuce. Cook all poultry to 165 degrees F. Measure in the thickest part. Do not let the thermometer tip touch bone or the pan.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Toss the filling in a bowl first so the dressing is even. If the wrap tears, roll it in parchment or foil to hold it.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
