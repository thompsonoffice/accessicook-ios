Leftover Pizza Reheat
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A method for reheating leftover pizza so the crust firms up and the toppings reach a safe temperature. Leftovers should be reheated to 165 degrees F. A skillet or an oven works better than a microwave if you want a less soggy crust, though the microwave is fine when speed matters more than texture.

SERVINGS
1 or more slices

TOTAL TIME
About 10 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans
- A talking or instant-read food thermometer

INGREDIENTS
- Leftover pizza slices, kept refrigerated
- A few drops of water, only if you use a covered skillet and the crust is very dry

TACTILE TIPS
Refrigerated pizza feels firm and the cheese is set. After reheating, the cheese should feel melted again, the crust should feel hot, and the toppings should feel steaming hot, 165 degrees F in the thickest topping, especially if the pizza has meat.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Take the pizza out of the refrigerator and remove any wrapping.

2. For a skillet: set a skillet over medium-low heat.

3. Add the slice.

4. Cover the pan.

5. Heat 4 to 8 minutes, until the bottom feels crisp and the toppings are 165 degrees F.

6. For an oven or toaster oven: heat it to 375 degrees F.

7. Set the slice on a pan.

8. Heat 8 to 12 minutes, until the toppings are 165 degrees F and the crust feels hot and firmer.

9. For a microwave: set the slice on a microwave-safe plate.

10. Heat 30 to 60 seconds, then in short bursts, until the toppings are 165 degrees F. The crust will stay softer this way.

11. Let the slice sit 1 minute, then serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The toppings should feel steaming hot, 165 degrees F. You should smell warmed tomato, cheese, and crust, not burning. The crust should feel hot all the way through, not icy or cold in the center of a thick slice.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The cheese should look melted again, and the toppings should look hot, with a little steam. The crust should look slightly crisp in a skillet or oven, or softer in a microwave. It should not look scorched.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The cheese should look melted again, and the toppings should look hot, with a little steam. The crust should look slightly crisp in a skillet or oven, or softer in a microwave. It should not look scorched.

SAFETY
Leftover pizza should have been refrigerated within 2 hours of the original meal. Reheat to 165 degrees F. Use mitts. Cheese and sauce can be hotter than the crust. Do not leave reheated pizza out for another long stretch. Eat it or return leftovers to the refrigerator. Cook casseroles to 165 degrees F in the center.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
A dry skillet is usually enough. A drop of water under the lid makes a little steam, which helps the cheese melt, but too much water softens the crust. This method is also linked from the Sheet-Pan Pizza recipe in the Supper chapter.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
