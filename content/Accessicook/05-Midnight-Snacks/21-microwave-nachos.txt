Microwave Nachos
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A single-plate nacho snack made in the microwave. This is faster than oven nachos and is meant for a small serving. Microwave power varies, so start with a short time and add more in 20-second bursts.

SERVINGS
1 or 2 as a snack

TOTAL TIME
About 5 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A microwave-safe mug or dish
- A microwave-safe cover or plate, vented
- A dry mitt or thick cloth for the hot dish

INGREDIENTS
- 2 large handfuls of tortilla chips, enough to cover a microwave-safe plate in a single loose layer
- 3/4 cup (three-quarters of a cup) shredded cheese
- Optional: drained salsa, sliced jalapenos, or leftover cooked beans

TACTILE TIPS
Use a microwave-safe plate, not metal. The cheese should feel fully melted, not still shredded in the center, and the plate will be hotter than you expect.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Spread the chips on a microwave-safe plate in a loose, even layer.

2. Sprinkle the cheese evenly over the chips.

3. Add any optional microwave-safe toppings.

4. Microwave 45 to 90 seconds, starting with 45 seconds, until the cheese is fully melted.

5. If the center is not melted, add 20 seconds at a time.

6. Stop as soon as the cheese is melted, or the chips can harden and scorch.

7. Let the plate sit 1 minute.

8. Add cold salsa or sour cream if you like, and serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The cheese should feel fully melted and stretchy. The plate will feel very hot. You should smell melted cheese and corn, not burning chips or a hot-plastic smell. If you smell scorching, stop.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The cheese should look melted and slightly glossy, not still shredded, and the chips should not look browned or dry at the tips.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The cheese should look melted and slightly glossy, not still shredded, and the chips should not look browned or dry at the tips.

SAFETY
Use a microwave-safe plate, never metal or a plate with metal paint. The plate and cheese will be very hot. Use a dry mitt or a pot holder to take the plate out. Let it sit 1 minute so the heat evens out and the cheese is less likely to burn your mouth. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
This does not reheat well, so make a small plate.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
