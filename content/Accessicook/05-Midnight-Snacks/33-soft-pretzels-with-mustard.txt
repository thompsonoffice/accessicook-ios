Soft Pretzels with Mustard
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Soft pretzels baked from store-bought dough, or reheated ready-made pretzels, served with mustard. A baking-soda bath, meaning a brief dip in hot water mixed with baking soda, can give dough pretzels a deeper brown and a more pretzel-like flavor, but it is optional.

SERVINGS
4 to 6 pretzels with store-bought dough, or as many as the package holds

TOTAL TIME
About 25 minutes with store-bought dough, or 10 minutes if you are only reheating ready-made pretzels

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans

INGREDIENTS
- 1 pound pizza dough, or a package of frozen soft pretzels with their own directions
- 2 tablespoons baking soda, optional, dissolved in 4 cups hot water, for a dip before baking dough pretzels
- 1 egg, beaten with 1 teaspoon water, optional, for brushing
- Coarse salt, for sprinkling
- Mustard, for serving

TACTILE TIPS
Dough should feel elastic and not ice-cold. A baked or reheated pretzel should feel set and slightly chewy, hot in the center, not doughy, and not hard as a cracker unless you prefer it very baked. Ready-made pretzels should be heated until the center is hot.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. If you are reheating ready-made pretzels, follow the package, often 10 minutes at 350 or 400 degrees F, until the centers feel hot and the outside feels slightly crisp.

2. Serve with mustard, and skip the shaping steps.

3. If you are using pizza dough, heat the oven to 425 degrees F.

4. Line a pan with parchment.

5. Divide the dough into 4 to 6 pieces.

6. Roll each piece into a long rope, then shape a pretzel, or a simple twist or stick if a pretzel shape is frustrating.

7. If you are using the baking-soda bath, dip each pretzel briefly in the hot baking-soda water, then set it on the pan.

8. If you are not, brush with the optional egg wash instead.

9. Sprinkle with coarse salt.

10. Bake 12 to 16 minutes, until the pretzels feel set and are browned, and the centers feel cooked, not doughy.

11. Cool 5 minutes.

12. Serve with mustard.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The pretzels should feel set, slightly chewy, and hot in the center, not doughy. You should smell toasted dough and salt, not raw flour or burning. Mustard should smell sharp and clean.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The pretzels should look golden to medium brown, not pale and not nearly black. The insides should look cooked and slightly chewy, not raw.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The pretzels should look golden to medium brown, not pale and not nearly black. The insides should look cooked and slightly chewy, not raw.

SAFETY
Do not taste raw dough. Use mitts. If you use a baking-soda bath, the water is hot. Work slowly. If you use an egg wash, wash your hands after handling the raw egg, and do not eat raw egg wash. Coarse salt can roll off the pan, so work over the sink or the pan. Do not taste raw dough or batter that contains eggs or raw flour. Bake until the doneness cues in the steps are met. Reheat leftovers until they are steaming hot.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Butter brushed on after baking is a good finish. Leftover pretzels reheat at 300 degrees F until the centers are hot.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
