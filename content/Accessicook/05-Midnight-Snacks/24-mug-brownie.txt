Mug Brownie
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A small, fudgy brownie cooked in a microwave-safe mug. It should stay slightly soft in the center. Start with a short time, because a brownie can go from fudgy to dry very quickly.

SERVINGS
1 mug

TOTAL TIME
About 5 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A microwave-safe mug or dish
- A microwave-safe cover or plate, vented
- A dry mitt or thick cloth for the hot dish

INGREDIENTS
- 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder
- A pinch of salt
- 2 tablespoons oil or melted butter
- 3 tablespoons milk
- 1/4 teaspoon (a quarter teaspoon) vanilla extract
- Optional: 1 tablespoon chocolate chips

TACTILE TIPS
The batter should feel thick and smooth, with no dry cocoa in the corners of the mug. The cooked brownie should feel set at the edges and slightly soft and fudgy in the center, not wet with raw batter, and not dry all the way through.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Mix the flour, sugar, cocoa, and salt in a microwave-safe mug at least 12 ounces in size.

2. Add the oil or butter, milk, and vanilla.

3. Stir until the mix feels even, including in the corners.

4. Stir in the optional chips.

5. Microwave 45 to 75 seconds, starting with 45 seconds, until the edges feel set and the center feels slightly soft, not wet with raw batter.

6. If the center is still wet batter, add 10 seconds at a time.

7. Let the mug sit 1 minute, then eat with a spoon.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The edges should feel set. The center should feel slightly soft and fudgy, not wet with raw batter. You should smell chocolate, not raw flour or burning. The mug will be very hot.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The brownie should look set at the edges and slightly glossy and soft in the center, not wet and not dry and cracked all over.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The brownie should look set at the edges and slightly glossy and soft in the center, not wet and not dry and cracked all over.

SAFETY
Use a microwave-safe mug, never metal. Do not taste raw batter. Use a pot holder to take the mug out. Let it sit 1 minute. Melted chocolate is very hot. Do not taste raw dough or batter that contains eggs or raw flour. Bake until the doneness cues in the steps are met. Reheat leftovers until they are steaming hot.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
A small scoop of ice cream on top is a good late-night finish, added after the mug has cooled 1 minute. Eat this at once.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
