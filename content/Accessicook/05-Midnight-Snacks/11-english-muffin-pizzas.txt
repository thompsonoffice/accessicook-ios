English Muffin Pizzas
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Split English muffins topped with sauce and cheese, then baked or toasted until the cheese melts and the edges are crisp. This is a late-night pizza when you do not want to make a full sheet-pan pizza. See also Leftover Pizza Reheat in this chapter, and Sheet-Pan Pizza in the Supper chapter.

SERVINGS
2 to 4 small pizzas

TOTAL TIME
About 15 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans

INGREDIENTS
- 2 English muffins, split
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) pizza sauce or marinara
- 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
- Optional toppings: sliced olives, cooked peppers, or already-cooked meat

TACTILE TIPS
The muffin cut-sides should feel slightly dry and toasted before you add a lot of sauce, or they can get soggy. The cheese should feel fully melted, and the muffin edges should feel crisp.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oven to 400 degrees F, or use a toaster oven.

2. Split the muffins and set them cut-side up on a pan.

3. Toast them 3 to 4 minutes, until the cut sides feel slightly dry and warm.

4. Add a thin layer of sauce, then cheese, then any optional toppings.

5. Keep toppings light.

6. Bake 6 to 10 minutes, until the cheese is fully melted and bubbling, and the edges feel crisp.

7. If you add leftover cooked meat, the topping should reach 165 degrees F.

8. Cool 1 or 2 minutes, then serve.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The cheese should feel fully melted. The edges should feel crisp. You should smell toasted wheat, tomato, and cheese, not burning bread. Leftover meat, if used, should feel steaming hot, 165 degrees F.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The cheese should look melted and bubbling, with a few light brown spots, and the muffin edges should look toasted, not pale and not burnt.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The cheese should look melted and bubbling, with a few light brown spots, and the muffin edges should look toasted, not pale and not burnt.

SAFETY
Use mitts. The pan and melted cheese are very hot. Use a fork or a spatula to move the muffins. If you use a toaster oven, make sure toppings cannot drip onto the heating element. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
A little dried oregano or a pinch of red pepper flakes is a good extra. A pinch is a small amount you can hold between two fingers, about 1/8 teaspoon (an eighth of a teaspoon). These are best eaten at once.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
