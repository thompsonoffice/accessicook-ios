ACCESSICOOK
Oven Fries
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Potato sticks tossed with oil and baked until they are crisp at the edges and tender inside. This is a late-night fry when you do not want a pot of hot oil. Cut the sticks as even as you can so they finish together. See also French Fries in the Misc chapter for a slightly more formal side-dish version.

SERVINGS
2 to 3 as a snack, or 4 as a side

TOTAL TIME
About 40 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A stable cutting board with a damp towel underneath
- A sharp chef's knife
- A cut-resistant glove for the hand that holds the food
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans

INGREDIENTS
- 2 medium russet potatoes, scrubbed
- 1 1/2 tablespoons (one and a half tablespoons) oil
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) salt, plus more to taste
- Optional: 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) paprika or garlic powder

TACTILE TIPS
Even sticks, about 1/4 to 1/3 inch (a quarter to a third of an inch) thick, cook more evenly. After baking, the edges should feel crisp, and the centers should feel tender, with no hard, raw core. A fry should bend a little, then break, not feel leathery or soggy.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oven to 425 degrees F.

2. Line a sheet pan with parchment, or oil the pan.

3. Cut the potatoes into even sticks on a stable board, using a cut-resistant glove on the hand that holds the potato if you need it.

4. Toss the sticks with the oil, salt, and any optional seasoning, until they feel lightly coated.

5. Spread them in a single layer with space between them.

6. Crowding makes them steam and stay soft.

7. Bake 15 minutes.

8. Turn the sticks.

9. Bake 10 to 20 minutes more, until the edges feel crisp and the centers feel tender.

10. Taste one after a brief cool, and add salt if it needs it.

11. Serve at once.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The edges should feel crisp. The centers should feel tender. You should smell toasted potato, not burning oil or raw potato. A test fry should have no hard, raw core.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The fries should look golden with browned edges, not pale and not burnt. They should look dry-crisp on the outside, not wet and limp.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The fries should look golden with browned edges, not pale and not burnt. They should look dry-crisp on the outside, not wet and limp.

SAFETY
Use a stable board and a sharp knife, slowly. Use mitts. The pan is very hot. Let a test fry cool a few seconds, because the inside holds heat. Do not deep-fry in this recipe. This dish has no raw meat. Still keep perishable ingredients cold. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Soaking the cut potatoes in cold water 15 minutes, then drying them very well, can make them a little crispier. Ketchup, Ranch Dip, or the cheese sauce in the Misc chapter can go on the side. See also Chili Cheese Fries in this chapter.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
