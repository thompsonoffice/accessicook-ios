ACCESSICOOK
Cleaning Up Safely
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Cleanup is part of cooking, not an afterthought. A clear sink and a known place for knives prevent cuts. A clean board and thermometer prevent the next meal from picking up bacteria from the last one. This chapter is a short, practical closing routine.

KNIVES AND SHARP TOOLS
Wash knives one at a time, by themselves, not loose in a sink full of soapy water. Set the knife on the counter, wash the blade with the sharp edge turned away from you, dry it, and put it away in a block, sheath, or magnetic strip. The same rule applies to vegetable peelers, box graters, and food-processor blades.

CUTTING BOARDS AND THERMOMETERS
Wash boards, tongs, and thermometers with soap and hot water after they touch raw meat, poultry, or eggs. If you used a board for raw chicken, do not slice bread on it until it has been washed. Some plastic boards can go in the dishwasher; wooden boards are usually hand-washed and dried upright.

THE STOVE, OVEN, AND AIR FRYER
Turn burners and the oven off before you clean around them. Let pans, air-fryer baskets, and oven racks cool until they are safe to handle. Wipe grease while it is still slightly warm if you can do that safely, because cold, hard grease is harder to remove, but never wipe a burner that is still on. Pour cooled bacon grease or frying oil into a jar, not down the sink.

THE MICROWAVE
Wipe splatters when the microwave is cool. A bowl of water heated until it steams can loosen dried food; use a mitt, and do not move a bubbling bowl carelessly.

SPILLS AND FLOORS
Wipe floor spills as soon as you can so they are not a slip hazard, especially oil and other grease. If oil spills on the floor, tell anyone else in the home, and stay off that spot until it is clean.

TRASH AND COMPOST
Tie bags before they overflow. Chicken trays and raw-meat wrappers should go straight to the trash so juices are not left on the counter. Wash your hands after handling trash.

A SHORT CLOSING CHECK
A good last pass is: heat off, knives put away, boards washed, food in the refrigerator, hot pans on a known landing zone, and your thermometer rinsed and back in its home. If you are cooking with a helper, do this check together out loud.
