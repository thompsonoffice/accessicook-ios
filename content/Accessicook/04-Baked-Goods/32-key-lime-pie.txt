Key Lime Pie
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A tart, creamy lime pie in a graham-cracker crust. The filling is sweetened condensed milk, lime juice, and egg yolks, and it sets in a short bake, then must chill. Regular lime juice works if you cannot find Key limes.

SERVINGS
8 servings

TOTAL TIME
About 35 minutes, plus several hours of chilling

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans

INGREDIENTS
- 1 1/2 cups (one and a half cups) graham-cracker crumbs
- 3 tablespoons sugar
- 5 tablespoons melted butter
- 1 can (14 ounces) sweetened condensed milk
- 4 large egg yolks
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) lime juice, plus 1 teaspoon lime zest if you like

TACTILE TIPS
Zest is the fragrant green outer skin of the lime, not the bitter white pith. The filling should feel smooth and pourable. After baking, it should feel just set, with a slight jiggle. After chilling, a slice should feel creamy and hold its shape.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oven to 350 degrees F.

2. Mix the crumbs, sugar, and melted butter until the mix feels like damp sand.

3. Press it into a 9 inch pie pan.

4. Bake the crust 8 minutes, then cool slightly.

5. Mix the condensed milk, egg yolks, lime juice, and optional zest until even.

6. Pour the filling into the crust.

7. Bake 12 to 16 minutes, until the filling is just set and still slightly jiggly in the center, not liquid.

8. Cool, then refrigerate at least 3 hours, until a slice holds its shape.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The filling should feel just set, with a slight jiggle, not slosh. You should smell lime and baked crust. After chilling, a slice should feel creamy and firm enough to cut.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The filling should look pale and just set, not browned and not liquid. After chilling, a slice should hold a clean edge.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The filling should look pale and just set, not browned and not liquid. After chilling, a slice should hold a clean edge.

SAFETY
Do not taste raw filling. It contains egg yolks. Use mitts. Cool, then refrigerate within 2 hours. Keep the pie refrigerated, and do not leave slices out more than 2 hours. Do not taste raw dough or batter that contains eggs or raw flour. Bake until the doneness cues in the steps are met. Reheat leftovers until they are steaming hot.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Whipped Cream from the Misc chapter is the usual topping. Bottled lime juice works, but fresh juice tastes brighter.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
