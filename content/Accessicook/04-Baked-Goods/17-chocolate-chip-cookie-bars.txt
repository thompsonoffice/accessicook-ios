Chocolate Chip Cookie Bars
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
All the flavor of chocolate chip cookies, pressed into a pan and cut into squares. This is easier than scooping individual cookies.

SERVINGS
16 bars

TOTAL TIME
About 40 minutes, plus cooling

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans

INGREDIENTS
- 2 1/4 cups (two and a quarter cups) all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
- 3/4 cup (three-quarters of a cup) brown sugar
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 2 cups chocolate chips

TACTILE TIPS
Melted butter should feel warm, not hot enough to cook the eggs.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oven to 350 degrees F.

2. Grease the pan, or line it with parchment, leaving a little overhang as a handle.

3. Mix the flour, baking soda, and salt.

4. In a second bowl, mix the melted butter and both sugars.

5. Add the eggs and vanilla.

6. Mix until even.

7. Add the dry mix and the chips.

8. Stir just until combined.

9. Spread the batter or dough evenly in the pan.

10. Bake 22 to 28 minutes.

11. A toothpick in the center should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter.

12. Cool in the pan before you cut, unless the recipe tips say otherwise.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The edges should feel set. The center should feel soft but not liquid. A toothpick should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter. You should smell toasted sugar and chocolate.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The top should look golden and set, with melted chips. The edges should pull slightly from the pan.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The top should look golden and set, with melted chips. The edges should pull slightly from the pan.

SAFETY
Do not taste raw batter. Use mitts. Cool before cutting so the bars do not fall apart. Do not taste raw dough or batter that contains eggs or raw flour. Bake until the doneness cues in the steps are met. Reheat leftovers until they are steaming hot.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Use a 9 by 13 inch pan for thinner bars, or a 9 by 9 inch pan for thicker ones and add a few minutes.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
