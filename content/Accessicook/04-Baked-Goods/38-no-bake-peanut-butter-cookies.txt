No-Bake Peanut Butter Cookies
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Stovetop cookies made with sugar, milk, peanut butter, and oats. They set as they cool, with no oven. Work quickly once the sugar mix has boiled, because the mixture thickens fast.

SERVINGS
About 24 cookies

TOTAL TIME
About 20 minutes, plus 20 minutes to set

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A large pot with a lid
- A wooden spoon or ladle

INGREDIENTS
- 2 cups sugar
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) milk
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) unsalted butter
- 1/4 cup (a quarter cup) unsweetened cocoa powder, optional
- 1 cup peanut butter
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3 cups quick oats
- A pinch of salt

TACTILE TIPS
The boiled mix is extremely hot. The finished cookies should feel firm enough to lift.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Line a baking sheet with parchment or wax paper.

2. Put the sugar, milk, butter, and cocoa, if using, in a pot.

3. Bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring.

4. When the mix reaches a full boil, boil 1 minute, still stirring.

5. Take the pot off the heat.

6. Stir in the peanut butter, vanilla, salt, and oats until evenly coated.

7. Drop rounded tablespoons onto the sheet.

8. Let the cookies sit until they feel set, about 20 minutes.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The cookies should feel firm on the outside and slightly soft inside after they cool. You should not feel a hot, liquid center.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The cookies should look matte and set, not wet and shiny.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The cookies should look matte and set, not wet and shiny.

SAFETY
The sugar mix is hotter than boiling water and can cause serious burns. Use a deep pot. Do not taste the boiling syrup. If you cook for someone with a peanut allergy, skip this recipe. Do not taste raw dough or batter that contains eggs or raw flour. Bake until the doneness cues in the steps are met. Reheat leftovers until they are steaming hot.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
If they do not set, the mix may not have boiled long enough. Boil a full minute the next time. Quick oats set better than old-fashioned oats here.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
