ACCESSICOOK
Banana Bread
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A moist loaf made with very ripe bananas. Quick bread means a loaf that rises with baking soda or baking powder, not yeast, so there is no rising time. The center takes longer than muffins, so test with a toothpick.

SERVINGS
1 loaf, about 10 slices

TOTAL TIME
About 1 hour 20 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans

INGREDIENTS
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) salt
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) cinnamon, optional
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) unsalted butter, melted, or oil
- 3/4 cup (three-quarters of a cup) sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 3 very ripe bananas, mashed
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

TACTILE TIPS
The mashed bananas should feel very soft and almost liquid in spots. The baked loaf should feel springy on top, and a toothpick in the center should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter. If the toothpick hits a melted chip, test again in another spot.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oven to 350 degrees F.

2. Grease a loaf pan, or line it with parchment.

3. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl.

4. Mix the wet ingredients in a second bowl, including any mashed fruit.

5. Add the wet mix to the dry mix.

6. Stir just until combined.

7. Spread the batter in the pan.

8. Bake 55 to 70 minutes.

9. A toothpick in the center of the loaf should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter. The top should feel set and the center should not feel wet.

10. Cool in the pan 10 minutes, then turn the loaf out to finish cooling.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The top should feel set. The center should not feel wet or collapse under light pressure. You should smell banana and toasted sugar, not raw flour. A toothpick in the center should come out with a few moist crumbs.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The top should look browned and slightly cracked, which is common. A toothpick in the center should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The top should look browned and slightly cracked, which is common. A toothpick in the center should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter.

SAFETY
Do not taste raw batter. Use mitts. The loaf pan is heavy and hot. Cool before you slice, or the loaf can crumble. Do not taste raw dough or batter that contains eggs or raw flour. Bake until the doneness cues in the steps are met. Reheat leftovers until they are steaming hot.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
One cup of chocolate chips or chopped walnuts can be stirred in. If the top browns before the center is done, tent the pan with foil, meaning rest a sheet of foil lightly on top. Rest means let cooked meat sit so the juices settle back into the meat. This loaf keeps 3 days wrapped at room temperature, or freeze slices.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
