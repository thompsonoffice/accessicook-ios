Vanilla Cupcakes
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
A smaller version of yellow cake, baked in a muffin pan. Fill the cups only about two-thirds full so the cupcakes have room to rise. Frost them when they are fully cool.

SERVINGS
12 cupcakes

TOTAL TIME
About 40 minutes, plus cooling

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans

INGREDIENTS
- 1 1/2 cups (one and a half cups) all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 teaspoons (one and a half teaspoons) baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon (a quarter teaspoon) salt
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) unsalted butter, softened
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) milk

TACTILE TIPS
Softened butter should give like cool clay. The batter should feel smooth. A toothpick in the center of a cupcake should come out clean or with a few crumbs. The tops should feel springy.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oven to 350 degrees F.

2. Line a muffin pan with paper cups, or grease the cups.

3. Mix the flour, baking powder, and salt.

4. Beat the butter and sugar until the mix feels fluffy. This is called creaming.

5. Add the eggs and vanilla, and beat until combined.

6. Add the flour mix in two parts, alternating with the milk, and stir just until you no longer feel dry flour.

7. Spoon the batter into the cups, filling each about two-thirds full.

8. Bake 16 to 20 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out clean or with a few crumbs, and the tops feel springy.

9. Cool in the pan 5 minutes, then move the cupcakes to a rack and cool completely before frosting.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The tops should feel springy. A toothpick should come out clean or with a few crumbs. You should smell vanilla and butter, not raw flour.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The tops should look risen and light golden. A toothpick should come out clean or with a few crumbs.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The tops should look risen and light golden. A toothpick should come out clean or with a few crumbs.

SAFETY
Do not taste raw batter. Use mitts. Cool completely before frosting. Do not taste raw dough or batter that contains eggs or raw flour. Bake until the doneness cues in the steps are met. Reheat leftovers until they are steaming hot.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
A simple swirl of Vanilla Frosting from the Misc chapter is enough. These freeze well unfrosted.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
