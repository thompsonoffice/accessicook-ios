Rice Cereal Treats
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Crisp rice cereal held together with melted marshmallows and butter. There is no oven and no eggs. Work quickly once the marshmallows melt, because the mix sets as it cools.

SERVINGS
16 squares

TOTAL TIME
About 15 minutes, plus cooling

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A large pot with a lid
- A wooden spoon or ladle

INGREDIENTS
- 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
- 1 package (10 ounces) marshmallows, or 4 cups mini marshmallows
- 6 cups crisp rice cereal
- A pinch of salt, optional

TACTILE TIPS
The melted mix should feel sticky, stretchy, and evenly coating the cereal. After cooling, a square should feel set and slightly chewy, not wet and not rock-hard.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Grease a 9 by 13 inch pan, or line it with parchment.

2. Grease a spatula too.

3. Melt the butter in a large pot over low heat.

4. Add the marshmallows.

5. Stir until they are fully melted and the mix feels smooth, with no lumps.

6. Take the pot off the heat.

7. Stir in the optional salt, then the cereal, until every piece feels coated.

8. Press the mix evenly into the pan with the greased spatula or a piece of parchment.

9. Press firmly, but do not crush the cereal flat.

10. Cool at least 30 minutes, then cut into squares.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The marshmallows should feel fully melted and smooth before you add the cereal. After cooling, a square should feel set and slightly chewy. You should smell vanilla marshmallow and toasted cereal, not burning sugar.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The treats should look pale and even, with visible cereal, not melted into a solid sheet. A cooled square should hold its shape.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The treats should look pale and even, with visible cereal, not melted into a solid sheet. A cooled square should hold its shape.

SAFETY
The melted marshmallow is extremely hot and sticky and can cause a serious burn. Keep wrists and fingers away from drips. Use a long-handled spoon. Do not taste the mix until it has cooled. The pot stays hot. Do not taste raw dough or batter that contains eggs or raw flour. Bake until the doneness cues in the steps are met. Reheat leftovers until they are steaming hot.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
A little vanilla or a handful of chocolate chips, stirred in after a brief cool, is a good extra. Store in a closed container at room temperature for 2 to 3 days. Do not refrigerate, or they can become hard.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
