ACCESSICOOK
Chocolate Cupcakes
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Moist chocolate cupcakes made with cocoa powder. They bake faster than a full cake, so start checking at the early time.

SERVINGS
12 cupcakes

TOTAL TIME
About 40 minutes, plus cooling

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans

INGREDIENTS
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/3 cup (a third of a cup) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon) salt
- 1 cup sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) milk
- 1/3 cup (a third of a cup) oil
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup (half a cup) hot water

TACTILE TIPS
The batter will feel thin after you add the hot water. A toothpick in a baked cupcake should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter. The tops should feel springy.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oven to 350 degrees F.

2. Line a muffin pan with paper cups, or grease the cups.

3. Mix the flour, cocoa, baking soda, salt, and sugar.

4. Add the egg, milk, oil, and vanilla, and stir until even.

5. Add the hot water carefully, and stir until the batter feels smooth.

6. Spoon the batter into the cups, filling each about two-thirds full.

7. Bake 16 to 20 minutes, until a toothpick in the center comes out with a few moist crumbs, and the tops feel springy.

8. Cool in the pan 5 minutes, then move to a rack and cool completely before frosting.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The tops should feel springy. A toothpick should come out with a few moist crumbs. You should smell chocolate and toasted sugar, not raw flour.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The tops should look set and slightly rounded. A toothpick should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The tops should look set and slightly rounded. A toothpick should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter.

SAFETY
Do not taste raw batter. Use mitts. Add the hot water slowly. Cool completely before frosting. Do not taste raw dough or batter that contains eggs or raw flour. Bake until the doneness cues in the steps are met. Reheat leftovers until they are steaming hot.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
Chocolate Frosting or a dusting of powdered sugar both work. Do not overbake, or the cupcakes will be dry.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
