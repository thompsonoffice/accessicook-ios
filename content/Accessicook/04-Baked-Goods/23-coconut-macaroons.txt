Coconut Macaroons
============================================================

INTRODUCTION
Chewy coconut cookies held together with egg whites and sugar. They are naturally flourless. Space them out so the edges can dry and toast.

SERVINGS
About 18 cookies

TOTAL TIME
About 35 minutes

DIFFICULTY
Beginner

EQUIPMENT
- Measuring cups and spoons with tactile markings
- A liquid measuring cup and a liquid level indicator when you are measuring liquids
- A tactile or talking timer
- A mixing bowl
- A whisk or fork
- A rimmed baking sheet or the baking dish named in the ingredients
- Parchment paper or foil, when useful
- Long oven mitts
- A reserved landing space for hot pans

INGREDIENTS
- 3 cups sweetened shredded coconut
- 3/4 cup (three-quarters of a cup) sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon (a quarter teaspoon) salt
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 3 large egg whites

TACTILE TIPS
The mix should feel sticky and hold a mound when you squeeze it. Shredded coconut is dry, stringy, and slightly sweet.

BEFORE YOU START
Read the whole recipe once before you turn on the heat. Set out the equipment and ingredients. Clear a landing space for hot pans. If you will be cutting, put a damp towel under the cutting board. If you are cooking with a helper, decide who will handle knives, the stove, and the thermometer before you start.

STEPS
1. Heat the oven to 325 degrees F.

2. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

3. Mix the coconut, sugar, salt, vanilla, and egg whites until the coconut feels evenly coated.

4. Roll the dough into 1 inch balls.

5. Place them on the sheet with space between them.

6. Bake 20 to 25 minutes.

7. Check at the early time. The edges should feel toasted and set. The centers should still feel moist.

8. Cool on the pan 2 to 3 minutes, then move the cookies to a rack or a plate to finish cooling.

HOW TO TELL WHEN IT IS DONE
Non-visual cues: The edges should feel set and a little crisp. The centers should feel moist and chewy. You should smell toasted coconut, not burning.

Visual cues for a sighted helper: The edges and tips of the coconut should look golden. The centers should still look moist, not dark brown.

FOR THE SIGHTED HELPER
Say what you see out loud, especially color and browning. Share any thermometer reading with your partner. Do not skip the temperature checks in the steps. Visual cues help, but they do not replace a thermometer for meat, poultry, egg dishes, or leftovers. The edges and tips of the coconut should look golden. The centers should still look moist, not dark brown.

SAFETY
Raw egg whites are in the mix, so do not taste the raw mixture. Bake until set. Do not taste raw dough or batter that contains eggs or raw flour. Bake until the doneness cues in the steps are met. Reheat leftovers until they are steaming hot.

TIPS AND VARIATIONS
For a chocolate dip, dip cooled macaroons in melted chocolate and let them set. If they spread too much, chill the scooped mounds 20 minutes before baking.

LEFTOVERS
Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours of cooking, or within 1 hour if the room is hotter than 90 degrees F. Use shallow containers so the food cools faster. Eat refrigerated leftovers within 3 to 4 days. Reheat leftovers to 165 degrees F. Bring leftover soups, sauces, and gravies back to a boil.
