Accessicook

Cookbook for blind cooks. For iPhone, iOS 17 or later. Coming to the App Store.

Accessicook is a cookbook written for blind and low-vision cooks, and for the people who cook alongside them. It has 327 recipes and 13 Kitchen Basics lessons, all built into the app. It works offline and is designed around VoiceOver.

Written To Be Heard

Every recipe uses the same headings in the same order, with one action per numbered step and plain language. Cooking terms are explained the first time they appear. Measurements and temperatures are written out, such as "1/2 teaspoon (half a teaspoon)" and "165 degrees F", so they read cleanly with a screen reader. Doneness cues come first by sound, smell, texture, and time, with visual cues included for a sighted helper. Food-safety temperatures follow USDA guidance.

What'S Inside

  • Kitchen Basics: 13 short lessons on setting up, measuring by touch, knife safety, using the stove and oven, and more
  • Breakfast: 40 recipes
  • Lunch: 55 recipes
  • Supper: 73 recipes
  • Baked Goods: 58 recipes
  • Midnight Snacks: 36 recipes
  • Misc (sides, sauces, appetizers, drinks, and staples): 65 recipes

Features

  • Browse by category, with a Start here lesson
  • Full-text search across titles, ingredients, and steps. Every word you type must match, and the number of results is spoken when you pause typing.
  • Recipe pages with real headings, so the VoiceOver headings rotor can jump to Ingredients, Steps, Safety, and the rest
  • Cook step by step: one step at a time with large Next and Previous buttons. With VoiceOver on, each new step is read to you. The screen stays awake while you cook.
  • An optional timer on any step that states a time, such as "5 minutes". You can pause it, add a minute, or cancel it. It runs while the app is open.
  • Favorites and recently viewed recipes, saved only on your iPhone
  • Share any recipe as plain text
  • A text size setting that goes up to the largest accessibility size. Bold Text, Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion, and Dark Mode follow your iPhone settings.

Private And Offline

No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking, and no in-app purchases. Accessicook collects no data.

Honest Notes

This is everyday American home cooking. Deep frying is left out on purpose for safety. A few breads start from store-bought dough. The recipes have not been individually kitchen-tested, and the app has not been formally screen-reader tested. The recipes were written with AI assistance and reviewed for consistency.
